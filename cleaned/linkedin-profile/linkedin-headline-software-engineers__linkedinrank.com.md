# 50+ LinkedIn Headline Examples for Software Engineers
Software engineering is one of the most competitive fields on LinkedIn. Recruiters search for specific technologies, frameworks, and specializations. A generic headline like “Software Engineer” gets lost in millions of similar profiles. This guide provides **50+ proven headline examples** organized by specialization, plus the formula to write your own.
Looking for examples from other industries?
## The Software Engineer Headline Formula
[Specialization] Engineer | [Top 2-3 Technologies] | [Company or Focus Area]
Include the technologies recruiters actually search for.

## Headlines to Avoid as a Software Engineer
✗
“Software Engineer”
Too generic. 5M+ people share this exact headline. Add your specialization and tech stack.
✗
“Coder | Tech Enthusiast | Problem Solver”
Buzzwords without specificity. Recruiters search for technologies, not personality traits.
✗
“SDE-2 at [Company]”
Internal levels mean nothing to external recruiters. Use industry-standard titles.
✗
“Passionate about building things”
Describes every engineer. State what you build and with what tools.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Should I list my tech stack in my headline?
Yes, include your top 2–3 technologies. Recruiters filter by specific technologies. "React & TypeScript" in your headline directly impacts whether you appear in search results.
### Should I include my company name?
If your company is well-known, yes | it adds credibility. If not, use the space for your specialization or a value statement instead.
### How specific should I be?
As specific as possible while remaining accurate. "Frontend Engineer | React & TypeScript" is far more effective than "Software Developer" for recruiter searches.
