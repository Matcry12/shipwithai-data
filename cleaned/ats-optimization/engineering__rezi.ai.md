# Engineering

## Common Accounting and Finance Job Responsibilities

- Prepare and analyze financial statements (e.g., balance sheet, income statement, cash flow statement).
- Maintain general ledger accounts and reconcile bank statements.
- Manage accounts payable and receivable.
- Perform budgeting and forecasting activities.
- Analyze financial data to identify trends and make recommendations.
- Prepare tax returns and other regulatory filings.
- Provide financial guidance and support to management.
- May perform internal audits and ensure compliance with accounting standards.
- Stay up-to-date on accounting laws and regulations.

## Accounting and Finance Resume Skills

Blend technical proficiency with strategic acumen. Showcase not only your ability to handle numbers, but your ability to influence decision-making and drive business growth.

### Hard skills

- Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (GAAP)
- International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS)
- Financial accounting software (e.g., QuickBooks, Oracle NetSuite)
- Financial modeling and forecasting
- Variance analysis
- Cost accounting
- Tax accounting
- Internal controls
- Audit procedures

### Soft skills

- Attention to detail
- Business acumen
- Communication
- Collaboration
- Critical thinking
- Decision-making
- Problem-solving

- Time management

## Examples of Accounting and Finance Resume Achievements

**Increasing profitability:**quantify a specific action you took that improved profitability.**Enhancing financial reporting:**highlight improvements to reporting efficiency or accuracy.**Resolving financial discrepancies:**showcase the result of fixing a discrepancy.**Improving financial controls:**emphasize your risk management and internal controls expertise.**Exceeding financial targets:**quantify your success in achieving financial goals.**Streamlining financial processes:**showcase your efficiency improvements to certain systems and processes.**Providing insightful financial analysis:**highlight the impact of your analysis.**Managing complex financial transactions:**demonstrate your ability to handle intricate financial matters.**Mentoring junior staff:**emphasize your leadership and knowledge-sharing skills.
