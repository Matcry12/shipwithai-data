## What Makes Executive Resumes Different
**Strategic vs. Tactical:** While mid-level resumes showcase project execution, executive resumes demonstrate strategic vision, organizational transformation, and business impact measured in revenue, market share, and shareholder value.
**Stakeholder Communication:** Your resume will be reviewed by board members, investors, and search committees. It must speak their language: P&L ownership, EBITDA improvement, market positioning, M&A leadership.
**Personal Brand:** At the executive level, you are selling your leadership philosophy and strategic perspective, not just your experience.

## FAQs: Executive Resumes
### How long should an executive resume be?
Two pages is standard for executives. The depth of experience and impact requires more space than entry-level roles. However, every word must earn its place – no filler.
### Should executives use a summary or objective?
Always a summary, never an objective. Executives do not need to explain what they want – they need to communicate what they deliver. Your summary is your strategic positioning statement.

### How far back should employment history go?
