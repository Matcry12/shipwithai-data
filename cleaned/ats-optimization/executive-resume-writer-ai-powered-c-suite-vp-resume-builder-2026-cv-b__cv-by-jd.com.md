# Executive Resume Writer

Command attention from executive recruiters and boards with a resume that showcases your strategic impact, leadership vision, and measurable business outcomes.

## What executive resumes must convey

### Business Impact

Revenue growth, cost savings, market share gains, M&A outcomes—quantified results that moved the needle.

### Strategic Vision

How you shaped company direction, entered new markets, led transformation initiatives, or repositioned brands.

### Leadership Scope

Team sizes, P&L responsibility, budget authority, cross-functional influence, board interactions.

### Change Management

Turnarounds, digital transformations, culture shifts, operational excellence—how you led change at scale.

### Industry Authority

Awards, speaking engagements, board seats, publications—credibility signals beyond the day job.

### Executive Presence

Tone, format, and structure that reflect seniority—concise, confident, results-first.

### Impact-Focused Writing

AI rewrites your experience to emphasize strategic outcomes, P&L results, and board-level contributions.

### Executive Summary

Generate a powerful leadership profile that positions you for C-suite, VP, or Board roles.

## Executive Resume FAQ

### How long should my executive resume be?

Two pages is standard for C-suite and VP roles. Focus on the last 10-15 years and emphasize strategic, high-impact achievements.

### Do I need ATS optimization as an executive?

Yes. Many executive recruiters use ATS. Optimize keywords while maintaining executive tone—avoid keyword stuffing or junior language.

### Should I include early-career roles?

Briefly. Focus on recent leadership roles. Early roles can be summarized as "Early Career: [Company], [Role]" without full details.
