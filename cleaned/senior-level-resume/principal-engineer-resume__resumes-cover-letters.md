# How To Write a Principal Engineer Resume (With Example)
Written by
Indeed Editorial Team
Updated December 19, 2025
### On this page


### On this page


A principal engineer is an engineering professional with many years of industry experience who oversees projects and members of their engineering team. If you're applying for a job as a principal engineer, knowing how to write a resume that highlights your skills and experience can help hiring managers notice your application. Learning about the process of writing an effective resume for this position may help you prepare an application that demonstrates your qualifications for the role.In this article, we provide seven steps for how to write a principal engineer resume, review a list of principal engineer skills and explore a resume template and a sample to help you write your own.

## How to write a principal engineer resume
Here are seven steps for how to write an effective principal engineer resume:
### 1. Read the job listing thoroughly
When preparing a job application, start by reviewing the job listing thoroughly. By reading the listing, you can learn about the needs of the position, including the hiring manager's preferences and requirements. The better you understand the expectations of the position, the more you can match your skills and experiences to the role. For example, principal engineers often have many years of experience working in their particular field. They also need experience in leadership and project management. Review the job description to gain insight into the level of industry experience the hiring manager expects from candidates.Related: [How To Tailor Your Resume to a Job Description](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/resumes-cover-letters/match-resume-with-job-description)
### 2. Format your resume
Create an outline and format your resume before you start adding information. You can either create your own outline or use a template to ensure you have all the necessary sections. [While developing an outline](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/resumes-cover-letters/resume-format-guide-with-examples), consider how much content to include on your resume and how to make the best use of the space available. A typical resume is about a page long but principal engineers often have many years of experience. Because of this, you may need to use strategic formatting and effective organization to fit your content on the page.For example, one way to improve the formatting of your resume is to create section headings. Format your headings using a standardized style, such as applying boldface or italics. Styling your headings with consistent formatting makes them easier for the reader to identify at a glance, which can improve the functionality and appearance of your document.
### 3. Write a targeted professional summary
After choosing a template, creating an outline or formatting your resume, [write a professional summary](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/resumes-cover-letters/resume-summary-examples) to introduce yourself. A professional summary is a brief statement that's typically two to three sentences long. You can use your summary to state how many years of industry experience you have, what your career goals are and what skills you can bring to your next position. Since principal engineers often need experience in their field, you may want to describe both your years of leadership experience and your amount of industry experience.If you have industry experience but not principal engineer experience, you can use your professional summary to highlight what qualities make you a good candidate for the job. You can state that you have leadership experience in other roles, such as being a project manager or mentor to junior engineers. Expressing these details shows that you have transferrable leadership skills that you can apply to the principal engineer position.Related: [A Complete Resume Summary Guide (40+ Examples)](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/resumes-cover-letters/writing-a-resume-summary-with-examples)
### 4. Outline your professional history
Include a section on your resume to detail your work history. It's common to include two or three of your most recent or relevant work opportunities on your resume. For each entry, provide details about your official job title, the name of the company you worked for, its location and your dates of employment. Also, give a list of three to five core duties you performed in the role.Try to match the responsibilities you include to the needs of the position to which you're applying. As an example, if the job listing states a need for an experienced principal engineer with strong design skills, you can emphasize both your experience and design aptitude in your work history section. You can add details about your experience with project design or describe a time when your design won an award.Related: [How To Describe Your Work Experience on Your Resume (With Examples)](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/resumes-cover-letters/describe-your-work-experience-sample)
### 5. List your skills
Principal engineers use a combination of [technical skills](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/resumes-cover-letters/technical-skills) and soft skills within their roles. They rely on technical skills to accomplish tasks, such as research and design. If you have experience with computer-aided design (CAD) software, tell the reader what programs you're proficient in using. You can also describe any heavy machinery or design techniques you know how to use. Additionally, they need soft skills to manage their time well and lead their teams successfully. You may add soft skills like leadership, time management and attention to detail to your skills list.Related: [12 Essential Engineering Skills for Your Resume](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/resumes-cover-letters/essential-engineering-skills)
### 6. Describe your education
Engineers typically have a bachelor's degree or higher to qualify for their roles. Some companies may prefer to hire principal engineers with advanced degrees, such as a master's degree in engineering, project management or another related subject. Make sure to add a section to your resume to show that you have the right educational credentials to qualify for the role. State the title of your degree and the name of the institution from which you graduated, using reverse chronological order if you have multiple degrees. If you graduated within the last three years, also list your year of graduation.Related: [How To List Education on a Resume](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/resumes-cover-letters/how-to-list-education-on-a-resume)
### 7. Include additional certifications
Near the end of your resume, include a section that details your certifications. Depending on your state and industry, a principal engineer position may require a Professional Engineer (PE) certification. And some employers may prefer candidates with other certifications that relate to the position. For example, a certificate in project management or leadership can be a great addition to your application as a principal engineer. These certificates show that you have specialized training related to leading groups and overseeing projects. Although not required, additional certification can demonstrate your value as a candidate.Related: [How To List Certifications on Your Resume in 5 Steps](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/resumes-cover-letters/list-certifications-on-resume)

## Principal engineer skills to include in your resume
Here are a few examples of skills to include on your principal engineer resume:
### Project management
Principal engineers often lead projects on behalf of their clients or supervisors. When managing projects, principal engineers may apply skills like time management, organization, risk management and leadership. These abilities help them create project plans that account for deadlines, budget limitations and their team's capacity to fulfill the project requirements. Showing you have good project management skills on your resume demonstrates that you have the right leadership and management qualities to oversee engineering teams while directing projects.Read more: [How To Improve Project Management Skills (With Additional Tips)](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/career-development/how-to-improve-project-management-skills)
### Analytical thinking
Analytical thinking is the ability to identify challenges, gather data and develop effective solutions. Principal engineers apply their analytical thinking skills to understand how their project solves problems for their clients. They also assess their options for designing equipment or products that meet their clients' needs while staying within their budgetary and deadline expectations.Read more: [Analytical Skills: Definitions and Examples](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/resumes-cover-letters/analytical-skills)
### Attention to detail
Good attention to detail allows principal engineers to account for diverse factors when planning and conducting projects. For example, principal engineers use attention to detail to understand the individual needs and concerns of their clients or supervisors during the project planning phase. By accounting for all the details a project involves, they can create comprehensive plans that allow their team to work effectively toward meeting client goals. Additionally, engineers use attention to detail when designing products. They pay close attention to how the mechanical features of the product operate to ensure it works as intended.Read more: [Attention to Detail: Definition and Examples](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/career-development/attention-to-detail)
### Communication
Principal engineers may work with various stakeholders and team members throughout the workday, so they need good communication skills to collaborate with others. They may use strong verbal communication skills during team or client meetings, and they might use written communication skills to draft progress reports and answer emails. When principal engineers communicate effectively, it can help their team understand their expectations and ensure stakeholders receive regular updates about current projects.Read more: [Communication Skills for Career Success](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/resumes-cover-letters/communication-skills)
