## What is an entry-level engineer resume?
An entry-level engineer resume is a document that professionals who are starting their career as an engineer can use to find a job. Since you likely have little work experience in the engineering field, it's important to focus on the skills and education that make you a qualified candidate. Typically, entry-level engineer resumes follow the standard resume format, which is one page that includes sections on your experience, education and skills. ## What to include on an entry-level engineer resume
Here are several items that you can consider including in your resume:
  * Contact information: Include your full name, phone number, email address and links to any professional networking sites.
  * Objective statement: This outlines your skills, education and goals to give employers a better idea of your background and qualifications.
  * Experience: It's useful to include any volunteer work, internships and work experience, detailing your duties and responsibilities in each position.
  * Education: List your engineering education, including information about your coursework, extracurricular activities and the name of each school.
  * Certifications: Be sure to include the titles of any engineering certifications you have and the name of the certifying institution.
  * Skills: Emphasize your skills that are useful in engineering, like problem-solving, equipment knowledge and math skills.

## How to create an entry-level engineer resume
Use these steps to guide you in creating an entry-level engineer resume:
### 1. Include your contact information
At the top of your resume, include your full name, phone number, email, location and links to professional networking websites. Placing this information in the header of your resume gives employers an easy way to reach you to schedule an interview or request further information from you.
### 2. Create an objective statement
An entry-level objective statement should detail your skill, education, any relevant experience you have in engineering and your professional goal, in one to two sentences. Here are some examples of entry-level engineer objective statements:
  * Recent mechanical engineer graduate looking to use strong problem-solving and analytical skills to secure a position as an associate engineer at a large-scale engineering firm.
  * Recent graduate with a bachelor's degree in electrical engineering, looking to secure a position as an entry-level engineer in the electrical industry and use my math skills and technical knowledge to grow my experience.

Related: [How To Write an Entry Level Resume Objective (With Examples)](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/resumes-cover-letters/entry-level-resume-objective)
### 3. Describe work experience
List your previous work experience, including internships and volunteer work. Include the name of the organization and the duration of your work. You can use bullet points to describe your duties, beginning each bullet point with an action verb and ending with a description of the task. For example, you might include, "Performed risk analysis of electrical work" or "Collaborated with senior engineers on internship project."
### 4. Include educational background
Depending on the position, employers may require candidates to have a bachelor's or master's degree in a field related to engineering, so it's essential to include information on your education. Add the name of the institution, the degree you received and the date you graduated.
### 5. Add certifications
Some employers may require candidates specialized engineering certifications, so be sure to include the name of your certifications, the date you received it and the name of the institution you received it from.
### 6. List relevant skills
Include skills that show hiring managers you can be a valuable asset to their company. Here are several skills you might include in your resume:
  * Computer science
  * Problem-solving
  * Equipment knowledge
  * Analysis
  * Leadership
  * Teamwork
  * Attention to detail

## Tips for writing an entry-level engineer resume
Here are several tips to help you as you write your resume:
### Proofread
Be sure to proofread your resume before submitting it to find any spelling errors or formatting issues. Sending in an error-free resume is a great way of demonstrating organizational skills and showing your determination to get an interview. It's useful to have a friend read through your resume to find any errors you might've missed.
### Tailor to your resume to each position
Try to tailor your resume to align with each position you apply for. For example, if a job description states that a company is looking for a candidate with organizational skills, you might include in your objective statement that you have strong organization skills.Read more: [How To Tailor Your Resume To a Job Description (With Example)](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/resumes-cover-letters/tailoring-resume)
### Use keywords throughout your resume
Typically, hiring managers place keywords throughout a job description that they look for when reviewing resumes. It's useful to include these keywords to help your resume stand out. To find keywords, try looking for words and phrases that repeat throughout a job description. Here are some keywords that engineering job descriptions may include:
  * Analysis
  * Mechanical design
  * Product development
  * Risk analysis
  * Equipment knowledge
  * Project design
  * Materials testing
