## How to write Technical Lead Resume
Technical Lead role is responsible for technical, analytical, development, software, business, leadership, programming, design, java, sql.   
To write great resume for technical lead job, your resume must include: 
  * Your contact information
  * Work experience
  * Education
  * Skill listing


### Contact Information For Technical Lead Resume
The section contact information is important in your technical lead resume. The recruiter has to be able to contact you ASAP if they like to offer you the job. This is why you need to provide your: 
  * First and last name
  * Email
  * Telephone number


### Work Experience in Your Technical Lead Resume
The section work experience is an essential part of your technical lead resume. It’s the one thing the recruiter really cares about and pays the most attention to.   
This section, however, is not just a list of your previous technical lead responsibilities. It's meant to present you as a wholesome candidate by showcasing your relevant accomplishments and should be tailored specifically to the particular technical lead position you're applying to. The work experience section should be the detailed summary of your latest 3 or 4 positions. 
Representative Technical Lead resume experience can include:
  * Excellent communications skills and strong leadership experience
  * Five years’ experience in technical specialty. 3. Strong analysis and problem solving skills
  * Eight (8) years’ experience in technical specialty. Strong analysis and problem solving skills
  * Five (5) to Eight (8) years’ experience in technical specialty. Strong analysis and problem solving skills
  * Strong database modeling and development skills, experience with SQL (Sybase and/or SQL Server) and NoSQL databases (ElasticSearch, Cassandra or similar)
  * Software development experience with extremely strong skills in Unix and Java


### Education on a Technical Lead Resume
Make sure to make education a priority on your technical lead resume. If you’ve been working for a few years and have a few solid positions to show, put your education after your technical lead experience. For example, if you have a Ph.D in Neuroscience and a Master's in the same sphere, just list your Ph.D. Besides the doctorate, Master’s degrees go next, followed by Bachelor’s and finally, Associate’s degree. 
Additional details to include: 
  * School you graduated from
  * Major/ minor
  * Year of graduation
  * Location of school


These are the four additional pieces of information you should mention when listing your education on your resume.

When listing skills on your technical lead resume, remember always to be honest about your level of ability. Include the Skills section after experience.
