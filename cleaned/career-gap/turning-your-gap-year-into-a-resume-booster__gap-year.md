## 6 Tactics to Make a Gap Year Great for Your Resume
Taking a gap year doesn’t have to mean hitting pause on your ambitions. In fact, it can be one of the most productive and transformative decisions you make. A well-planned gap year allows you to explore the world, gain real-world experiences, and develop skills that make you stand out to employers.
Depending on how intentional you are with your gap year planning, you can compile many valuable assets for your upcoming career. For example, if you want to focus your gap year travel on research opportunities, then that should influence _where_ you choose to go. Similarly, if you’re like me and the entrepreneurial route is what’s in store for you, then you’ll probably need to [plan for expenses](https://www.goabroad.com/articles/gap-year/gap-year-budget-planning) to get your idea off the ground.
Regardless of the route you take, there are plenty of ways to make a gap year on your resume stand out to a potential employer in the future. Here are six of the best tactics to maximize your gap year:
### 1. Consider internships or apprenticeships.
![person by a table with laptop and papers](https://www.goabroad.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fimages.goabroad.com%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fv1%2Fimages2%2Fprogram_content%2FkKswL8y1VIweOdpgXAcWoeTtY8byqiKgL2gDlDD2.jpg&w=1920&q=75)
From theory to practice—internships bridge the gap. Real-world experience starts here
Internships and apprenticeships are where the rubber meets the road. They can certainly be competitive, but they also offer unparalleled opportunities to gain real-world experience in a professional setting. By taking on a gap year internship in your desired industry, you’ll go beyond classroom theory and engage directly with the practicalities of your field. This is exactly what a school will appreciate, and a future employer looking at a gap year on your resume even more so.
During your gap year internship you could be shadowing seasoned professionals, contributing to projects, or learning new technologies, all of which will help you professionally and personally.
[Finding the perfect internship](https://www.goabroad.com/gap-year/search/internships/gap-year-1) or apprenticeship starts with figuring out what you’re aiming for. Are you looking to dive into a specific field or just explore your options while picking up some professional skills? Once you’ve got a sense of direction, it’s time to do some investigative work. Don’t overlook local or international resources, like trade associations or [apprenticeship programs](http://apprenticeship.gov/) that may be more common in certain countries.
When it comes to your internship application, make sure it shines (and [your resume has all the right line items](https://www.goabroad.com/articles/gap-year/gap-year-resume-tips)!). Even if you don’t have tons of experience, don’t sweat it—highlight what you’ve done, whether that’s academic projects, volunteer work, or leadership roles. Tailor your resume and [cover letter](https://www.careertoolbelt.com/) to show hiring managers why you’re excited about the internship role and what you can bring to the table.
### Why It’s a Resume Boost
Internships and apprenticeships give you the chance to translate real-world accomplishments into resume-ready achievements. For example:
Instead of "Assisted in a marketing team," you could say something like: _Collaborated on a digital marketing campaign, optimizing ads to increase click-through rates by 15%_. This measurable impact showcases the value you brought to your role.
#### Applicable Majors
Choosing the right internship or apprenticeship can [significantly enhance your resume](https://www.goabroad.com/articles/gap-year/20-gap-year-activities), showcasing hands-on experience and industry connections.
  * **Business:** Let’s be honest, most internships can relate to business in some form or fashion. Opportunities in sales, marketing, or finance provide a firsthand look at corporate operations.
  * **Creative Arts:** Internships in media, advertising, or design give you a platform to apply your creativity while building a portfolio.


## 2. Try out volunteering.
![people unloading food from the back of a truck](https://www.goabroad.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fimages.goabroad.com%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fv1%2Fimages2%2Fprogram_content%2FeR7yozWzVpImqA06yUZya5Qjq8cB494aEtorEgw1.jpg&w=1920&q=75)
Give back and level up—volunteering makes a difference in more ways than one!
[Volunteering during a gap year](https://www.goabroad.com/gap-year/search/volunteering/gap-year-1) offers a meaningful way to make a difference while developing critical skills that employers value. These experiences help build empathy, leadership, and adaptability.
The first step is to align your interests with your goals. For example, if you’re passionate about education, search for teaching or tutoring roles where you can work directly with students. If environmental issues resonate with you, consider conservation programs focused on reforestation or marine ecosystems. Healthcare students can explore medical outreach initiatives or public health campaigns.
Once you’ve chosen a focus, you can [browse our gap year directory](https://www.goabroad.com/gap-year) to find ethical, well-reviewed programs. Pay close attention to program details, including costs, location, and support services like accommodation and cultural orientation. If affordability is a concern, research scholarships or [crowdfunding opportunities](https://www.fundmytravel.com/) to fund your volunteer work.
### Why it's a Resume Boost
Volunteering gives you the opportunity to craft compelling resume entries that highlight your leadership and **measurable** achievements. Instead of "English teacher for preteens," you could say something like: _Organized and led workshops for 150+ students in rural Thailand, increasing literacy rates by 25%_.
#### Applicable Majors
Volunteering builds valuable skills, expands professional networks, and demonstrates a commitment to community impact.
  * **Education and Linguistics:** Look for teaching or literacy-focused roles.
  * **Environmental Science and Biology:** Consider conservation, marine protection, or ecological restoration programs.
  * **Healthcare and Public Health:** Check out programs that involve medical outreach, health education, or public health initiatives.


## 3. Focus on skill development.
![person using laptop](https://www.goabroad.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fimages.goabroad.com%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fv1%2Fimages2%2Fprogram_content%2FDVzch6K863SSwjRqmQ3P3fJBTYz5VvnMlza2CyIH.jpg&w=1920&q=75)
Skill-building mode: ON. Learn, grow, and stand out in the job market.
Taking your gap year to acquire new skills is one of the most practical ways to prepare for your future career. Whether it’s [learning a new language](https://www.goabroad.com/articles/language-study-abroad/10-best-ways-to-learn-a-new-language), mastering a software program, or earning certifications, these activities demonstrate to employers that you’re proactive and eager to grow. Skill development isn’t just about technical expertise—it’s also a chance to showcase your adaptability and commitment to self-improvement.
If you’re interested in language acquisition, [immersive language learning programs](https://www.goabroad.com/gap-year/search/language-immersion/gap-year-1) are one of the most effective options. Spending six months in a French-speaking region of Canada or completing a [Spanish immersion course in Mexico](https://www.goabroad.com/gap-year/search/mexico/spanish/gap-year-1) will allow you to learn both language and cultural nuances. Additionally, hands-on workshops or trade schools provide excellent training for careers in fields like carpentry, culinary arts, or photography.
### Why it's a Resume Boost
Focusing on skill development during a gap year is ideal for creating resume entries that highlight initiative and tangible results. Instead of simply stating, “Completed a Python course,” you could say: _Completed a 12-week Python bootcamp, developing three web applications, including an interactive budgeting tool that tracks expenses and savings in real time._
#### Applicable Majors
Skill development opportunities help build expertise that strengthens a resume, enhances career prospects, and makes you stand out in a competitive job market.
  * **Computer Science and IT:** Consider coding bootcamps, data analysis, or software certifications.
  * **Business and Marketing:** Find digital marketing credentials, financial modeling, or project management certifications.
  * **Linguistics and Cultural Studies:** Explore immersion language programs or professional translation certifications.


## 4. Explore research opportunities.
![person using magnifying glass](https://www.goabroad.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fimages.goabroad.com%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fv1%2Fimages2%2Fprogram_content%2FtMm70ZNBE4iX0xGsxCX4PBbAuYQY8hGwidzMom5h.jpg&w=1920&q=75)
Curiosity fuels discovery. Dive into research and uncover new possibilities.
For students with a curious mind and an eye for problem-solving, dedicating your gap year to research can be a transformative experience. Whether it’s assisting with climate change studies in Australia, analyzing economic trends in South Africa, or contributing to healthcare innovations in Germany, research opportunities offer hands-on engagement in cutting-edge projects. These gap year experiences will allow you to develop technical expertise, critical thinking, and a deeper understanding of your field of interest.
Research roles are often associated with academic institutions, non-profits, or private organizations. Begin by identifying areas of study that align with your passions or career goals, such as environmental science, public health, or sociology. Some research programs include formal training in methodologies or tools, such as statistical analysis software or laboratory techniques, which can further enhance your skill set.
Be prepared for logistical requirements, such as [securing visas](https://www.goabroad.com/travel-resources/passports-visas) for international research roles or covering costs for specialized equipment or fieldwork. Many institutions provide grants or stipends for research assistants, so [explore funding options](https://www.goabroad.com/scholarships-abroad/gap-year) early in your gap year planning process.
### Why it's a Resume Boost
Research experience offers compelling resume entries by highlighting your analytical abilities and contributions to meaningful projects. For instance, _Conducted a three-month study on water quality in rural Kenya, collecting and analyzing 300+ samples to identify contamination trends and propose solutions to local officials._
This entry demonstrates technical expertise, problem-solving, and the ability to work in challenging environments.
#### Applicable Majors
Engaging in research opportunities will enhance your resume by demonstrating analytical thinking, problem-solving, and technical expertise.
  * **STEM Fields:** Look for lab-based research or field studies in biology, chemistry, or environmental science.
  * **Political Science and Sociology:** Dig into policy analysis, social impact research, or data collection on cultural trends.
  * **Health Sciences:** Contribute to public health studies, clinical trials, or epidemiological research.


## 5. Look into teaching or tutoring opportunities.
![person teaching code](https://www.goabroad.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fimages.goabroad.com%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fv1%2Fimages2%2Fprogram_content%2FkYbM6LKoTXUSvUrgtMLw9DCAHvoB08ct6Gxd5u1q.jpg&w=1920&q=75)
Teach, inspire, and grow. Sharing knowledge is its own kind of adventure.
[Teaching or tutoring during your gap year](https://www.goabroad.com/gap-year/search/teach-abroad/gap-year-1) is a rewarding way to share your knowledge while gaining invaluable skills like communication, leadership, and adaptability. Whether it’s teaching English abroad, tutoring underserved students, or conducting workshops on specialized topics, these experiences provide direct, measurable impact that will make a gap year look great on your resume.
Teaching opportunities often fall into two categories: formal teaching roles and informal tutoring. For formal roles, [TEFL certifications](https://www.goabroad.com/tefl-courses) allow you to teach in countries like Japan, Spain, or Vietnam. These programs often include training, job placement assistance, and guidance on cultural integration.
Tutoring can involve offering one-on-one or small group lessons in subjects you’re passionate about. For example, a math major might tutor middle school students, or a music student might teach basic guitar lessons.
As with anything, you’ll want to research cultural norms and language basics for your destination. In addition to that being just a pragmatic thing to do, if you plan on teaching where you’re going, [cultural understanding will be even more important](https://www.goabroad.com/articles/benefits-of-cultural-awareness), as it could play a larger-than-normal role in how your lessons are absorbed.
### Why it's a Resume Boost
Teaching and tutoring roles translate easily into strong resume entries that showcase leadership and measurable outcomes. For example: "_Taught conversational English to 25+ high school students in Vietnam, improving language proficiency scores by 15% over three months."_
This highlights not just what you did but how you made a difference, demonstrating your ability to lead, educate, _and_ deliver results.
#### Applicable Majors
Pursuing teaching or tutoring opportunities can strengthen a resume by building essential communication, leadership, and instructional skills.
  * **Education and Child Development:** Teaching or tutoring roles focused on literacy, language, or STEM subjects.
  * **Foreign Languages and Cultural Studies:** Language instruction, conversational practice, or cultural exchange programs.
  * **STEM Fields:** Math, science, or coding tutoring for school-aged students or community learners.


## 6. Start entrepreneurial projects.
![two people sitting in front of a computer](https://www.goabroad.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fimages.goabroad.com%2Fimage%2Fupload%2Fv1%2Fimages2%2Fprogram_content%2F95Cf2mj24XRXonjnSZQnNijvvpDgJWysnbH4C3qJ.jpg&w=1920&q=75)
Start small, dream big. Entrepreneurship turns ideas into action.
Launching an entrepreneurial project during your gap year is bold–no doubt about it. Anyone I ever hired for a digital marketing role got a huge leg up if they had started their own business, blog, etc. To me, it not only shows initiative but, more importantly, it shows that they have been marketing themselves.
Entrepreneurial projects require a clear idea, careful planning, and execution. Start by identifying a problem you’re passionate about solving or a niche you want to explore. For example, you could develop a sustainable product for eco-conscious consumers, design an app that improves productivity, or launch a local service addressing unmet needs in your community.
Once you have your idea, create a roadmap. Research your market, determine your target audience, and build a business model. Platforms like Canva or Wix make branding and website creation accessible, while tools like Google Workspace or Trello can help you stay organized.
The key to success is adaptability—learn from challenges, refine your approach, and continuously seek feedback from mentors, peers, or potential customers.
### Why it's a Resume Boost
Entrepreneurial projects provide impressive resume entries by showcasing initiative, leadership, and results. For example: "_Founded and managed a small-scale e-commerce store selling handcrafted eco-friendly goods, achieving $10,000 in sales within six months and having 10% repeat customers.”_
#### Applicable Majors
Entrepreneurial projects showcase innovation, problem-solving, and leadership—key qualities that stand out on a resume.
  * **Business and Entrepreneurship:** Developing and managing small businesses or startups.
  * **Creative Arts and Design:** Launching a portfolio-based project, such as a photography business or Etsy shop.
  * **Engineering and IT:** Designing apps, prototypes, or digital tools to solve real-world problems.

Your gap year, your legacy. Make it meaningful and unforgettable.
Your [gap year](https://www.goabroad.com/gap-year) is more than just time away—it’s an opportunity to invest in yourself and your future. By choosing purposeful experiences, you can develop skills, expand your perspective, and craft resume entries that set you apart in the competitive job market.
Ultimately, a successful gap year isn’t just about what you do—it’s about how you grow. My suggestion is to grow intelligently and deliberately.
