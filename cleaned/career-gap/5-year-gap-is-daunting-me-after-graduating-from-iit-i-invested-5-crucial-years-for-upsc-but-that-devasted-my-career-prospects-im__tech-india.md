Hi there. Same story. 7 years gap, fresher with no work ex. Middle class parents in India are the best and the worst. Just depends on the case. If you dont succeed, they'd be the worst. Personal experience. I was at rock bottom till November 2023. Had skills on Data Analytics but wasn't getting offers simply due to the gap. Then my friends suggested to only focussing on stopping that gap. Number 1 priority. Because logically, recruiters are only seeing that on my CV right now. So I took up a job, in a very very unrelated field : US real estate, a start-up. Was on stipend as an intern, in a field I had zero knowledge about. But I have kept on persevering since day 1. And it's been 8 months. The reality that I'm no longer a fresher, don't have that GAP staring back at me so much gives me better sleep at night now. But yes, I had to work my ass off, in US timings. But I leveraged my analytical skills and critical thinking I took from UPSC prep to never stop questioning from my peers and seniors. Thus sole trait made me stand out among many other recruits that are my peers. Today, I can thankfully say that I got few of the best mentors in my company who NEVER shied away from answering my questions, because of which I grew. Proudly, I say that within 7 months, in my team, I am considered as the go-to person for resolving stuck issues within my product, after the team lead. I dabble with the Director and am handed projects which require people management. That's something I'm learning as well on the side. And I'm a girl, from North India, almost 30, and dealing with a lot of pressure to get married. So here goes the clichéd saying, if I can manage to get out of darkness, you can too. Start really small. Put your ego, your degree aside. Be ready to face anything for the upcoming few years, but be relentless. There's light definitely at the end of the tunnel. All this nowhere means I have it all figured out now. No. I still question myself if and till when I would wanna continue I'm this role, this company or domain. But yes, I still have a job over so many people currently sitting jobless and not getting calls. I will make switches, into the technical side or probably Project Management roles, but in time. (P.S. I started with a stipend of 20k only, but mainly due to my work ethic, my quality of work, I got a raise of 50% to 30k a month within 6 months. Purely because I was one of the members to grow my the product my team works on, for the company. ) So go get it dude!!
