Hey there, let me introduce myself first. I am a B.Tech (CSE) graduate and as a fresher, I have given many interviews for roles like ***frontend, backend, and full stack. Here is the resume that helped me to get a callback and start the interviews.***

Let's start with the ***full picture*** first and then I will ***explain each part's significance*** and how ***you can craft each word*** of this resume to work for you and make you ***stand out from the rest***. Also in the end, I will also attach the link to this resume template so you can use it to make your own.

Full Picture

#### 1. The Heading

I would suggest just sticking with ***Name, Mobile number, Email, and Important links (Github, Linkedin, Portfolio)(Make sure they are clickable)***. Don't worry if you don't have a portfolio website, GitHub will do just fine. ***Don't use your images in your resume***, the recruiter doesn't care how you look and ATS does have problems with images so images are a no-go.

#### 2. The Experience

![None](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:700/1*x6P41g1uWEuRsZitZotIuQ.png)

The Experience

Remember to fill in the points about what ***impact you created***, writing about how you worked on a certain tech stack will come at the end of the experience. ***Focus on the impact, what did you change, and what did you make better. Notice that I am using bold words to show the important things.***

***Start with action keywords*** like "Implemented, Migrated, Imporved" and ***end with the impact part***.

Try writing about actual development experience, if you have done other non-development internships or jobs, try not to include them or just don't keep them at the focus, ***try to show the more "relevant" experience for the role you are applying to and keep the focus on it. Keeping the focus means writing more about it.*** I did 2 internships but I kept the shorter one on the top as it's more relevant to the full stack role I was trying to apply to.

#### 3. THE PERSONAL PROJECTS

![None](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:700/1*e3pywNYwXnPn9OEG8QJ3Rw.png)

The personal Projects

***This was the holy grail of my resume***, whatever I did when I was learning and building projects I wrote it all down and later talked about it for 50% of the interview.

***Basically, I describe the features and utilities that I integrated into the app using what technology.*** Iwould suggest you write as much as you can about your personal projects and ensure everything that you write is concise and to the point.
> **Having internship experience is good but I think I learned the most when I was working on my personal projects.**

#### 4. Technical Skills

![None](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:700/1*V9MYrJ4jdousA-ObiLZFgA.png)

The Technical Skills

***Try to limit these to the things that you can answer any good question on*** and that too on the spot. The interviewer can try to ask an intermediate question based on these always keep that in mind.

The skills which I was not so confident in at that time, I added them in "Familiar with".

#### 5. The EXTRA Activities

![None](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:700/1*pJgGV1vqi5GlrXSmnaqGKg.png)

The Extra Activities

If you have participated and achieved a rank in some competition show it here, ***I think this section can also be skipped*** but if you have some good ranks, showing them would also help in the process.
> **Remember to keep everything technical, if it's not a technical achievement then it will be better not to add it as it won't hold any value and the recruiter might think you are just trying to fill the resume.**
