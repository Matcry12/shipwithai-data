#  5 Tech Resume Writing Tips for Remote Roles 
by Crossover  

![5 Tech Resume Writing Tips for Remote Roles](https://assets-us-01.kc-usercontent.com:443/7beb5311-75a4-0049-50f5-8f58fd55aba7/76f00f5d-e770-4b6d-b438-e7591ff481c5/Article_70.jpeg?fm=jpg&auto=format&w=800&h=500&fit=clip)
Looking for tech resume writing tips to help your application stand out from the pack? As more roles go fully remote, differentiating yourself from fellow tech applicants is key for career progression. Outdated as it is to have a resume in 2024, if you need one you might as well do it right! 
**This guide provides 5 killer**[**tech resume writing tips**](/resources/is-the-cover-letter-dead-what-works-instead)**for remote workers to help you showcase your skills and experience in a way that screams "hire me!"**
## **Why Your Tech Resume Needs a Makeover**
Before we dive into the nitty-gritty, it’s worth noting that the best resume writing tips in tech aren’t just about formatting -they’re about strategy, clarity, and knowing how to stand out in a sea of digital applications.
A staggering [98% of employees](https://buffer.com/state-of-remote-work/2023) would like to work remotely – that’s a lot of competition. 
[Your resume ](/resources/resume-screening-is-trash-the-facts)is a good opportunity to set yourself apart and convince your employer you’re the best person for the job. Just keep in mind that it needs to be _super clear._
With so many tech roles going remote, showcasing your ability to work independently, manage your time effectively, and communicate clearly online is essential. 
And with [75% of recruiters](https://www.jobscan.co/applicant-tracking-systems#:~:text=Do%20all%20recruiters%20use%20ATS,of%20big%20companies%20use%20it.) using Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to scan resumes - yours needs to be optimized to even get seen. This is only amplified because of the introduction of AI in recent years. 
Here’s how to turn your meh resume into a powerful tool that shows you're ready to thrive and make innovation happen for your new tech employer.
## **1. Put Your Remote-Ready Skills on Display**
  * **Adaptability:** Show that you can work independently and manage your time effectively – both important skills for [remote success.](/resources/the-unusual-leadership-soft-skills-powering-remote-success)
  * **Communication:** Emphasize your proficiency in [online communication tools](/resources/10-vital-tools-for-remote-leadership-8-ai-tools) and collaboration platforms. Think Zoom calls, Slack chats, and project management software.


## **2. Highlight Relevant Tech Skills and Stay Up-to-Date**
  * **Latest and Greatest:** Keep your resume updated with the latest [programming languages,](/resources/top-10-in-demand-programming-languages-for-2026-updated) [AI tools](/resources/why-ai-skills-will-set-you-apart) and software relevant to your field.
  * **Show You're Learning:** Don't forget to mention any recent certifications or courses that showcase your dedication to staying current with the latest advancements in your field.


## **3. Make Your Achievements Shine with Numbers and Impact**
  * **Quantify It:** Wherever possible, use data to showcase your accomplishments. For example, _"Increased website traffic by 40% through SEO optimization."_
  * **Focus on Outcomes:** Talk about the results of your projects, not just the tasks you completed. This shows the impact you can make, which is more valuable than experience.


## **4. Tailor Your Resume for Each Application**
  * **Keywords are Key:** Include relevant keywords from the job description to get past those pesky Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS). Not sure where to find them? Check LinkedIn and Indeed. 
  * **Match the Needs:** Tailor your skills and experiences to the specific requirements of the job you're applying for. Be the applicant that fits the position so well, they can’t ignore you. 


## **5. Keep it Clean, Crisp, and Concise**
  * **One Page is Enough:** Aim for a one-page resume that focuses on your most relevant experiences. That said, if you’re a pro or have over 10 years worth of experience 2-3 pages is standard.
  * **Easy on the Eyes:** Use a clean, professional layout with easy-to-read fonts and neat sections. Never use design work in your resume, fancy fonts or graphics that distract from your information. It won’t get you anywhere!


From highlighting your [remote-ready skills](/resources/10-tips-for-hiring-remote-software-developers) to customizing your resume for each application, these proven tech resume writing tips will help you showcase your unique talents so that you can land that interview. 
**Follow these 5 powerful writing tips and dramatically improve your chances of landing your dream job. PS: If you hate tech resumes, ditch them altogether and**[**apply for jobs**](/job-roles)**that don’t require such outdated (and unreliable) documentation.**
