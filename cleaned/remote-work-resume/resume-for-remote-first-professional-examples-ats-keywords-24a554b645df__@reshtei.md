### FAQ

**How long should a remote-first resume be?**
One page for entry-level roles. Two pages if you have 8+ years of remote experience. Keep it tight.

**Should I mention my home office setup?**
Only if the job description asks for technical requirements. Otherwise, save space for achievements.

**Do I need a separate skills section for remote tools?**
Yes. List them explicitly. ATS searches for exact tool names like "Slack" or "Asana."

**Can I use a remote address if I'm willing to relocate?**
Use your current address. Explain relocation willingness in your cover letter, not the resume.

**How do I show remote work experience if I only worked remotely during COVID?**
Frame it clearly. "Transitioned team of 12 to remote operations" shows leadership during that period.

**What if I have no formal remote work history?**
Highlight freelance projects, volunteer coordination, or online course completion. Anything that proves digital self-management.
