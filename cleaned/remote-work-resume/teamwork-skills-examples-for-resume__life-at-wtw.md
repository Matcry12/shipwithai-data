# Teamwork skills: how can you highlight them in your resume 
Set 4 2025 
In today’s increasingly collaborative work environment, whether remote, hybrid, or in-person, teamwork skills are not just desirable, they’re essential. 
As digital interactions and virtual collaboration become more and more common, the ability to work effectively with others has evolved to include a broader set of competencies. 
If you're looking for a career that moves your ideas to achievements and where your contributions matter, WTW is the collaborative company you should be applying to. 
In this article, we’ll share practical tips and examples of teamwork skills for a resume to help you stand out and reflect the team spirit you already bring to the table.

If you already have strong collaboration skills but aren’t sure how to present them, here are a few ways to bring your examples of teamwork skills for a resume to life. 
##    
7 practical tips to highlight teamwork skills on your resume 
### 1. Use strategic keywords 
Incorporate**terms that emphasize your teamwork abilities** , such as **collaboration, cooperation, team alignment, cross-functional teamwork, synergy and multidisciplinary teams**. These help recruiters quickly identify your strengths in working with others and support strong examples of teamwork skills for a resume. 
### 2. Start with action verbs 
Use dynamic verbs like **collaborated, co-led, partnered with, supported or contributed** to in your bullet points. These verbs show initiative and clearly communicate your role in team efforts.
### 3. Describe your roles and responsibilities 
Be specific about your position within the team. Explain how you contributed to the project’s success, whether you led meetings, coordinated tasks or supported teammates through challenges. 
### 4. Include interpersonal skills 
Teamwork thrives on soft skills. Highlight your strengths in effective communication, conflict resolution, empathy and active listening. These are essential for building trust and maintaining productive relationships.

### 5. Mention training and certifications 
> “If you’ve completed any training or earned certifications related to teamwork, like leadership courses or group dynamics workshops, don’t leave them out. They show your commitment to collaboration and can strengthen your examples of teamwork skills for a resume.” 
> **Veronica Lascano, Recruitment Lead**
### 6. Quantify your impact 
Whenever possible, add **metrics to show the results of your teamwork**. For example:   
“Collaborated with cross-functional teams to deliver a $2M digital transformation project ahead of schedule.” 
### 7. Tell the story behind success 
Go beyond listing tasks: describe the challenge, your role, the actions you took and the outcome. This storytelling approach brings your resume to life and shows how your teamwork skills directly contributed to results.
