## Frequently asked questions about creating a remote-proof resume to find your dream job.
Here are the FAQs on creating a CV suitable for remote work. What should you pay attention to? And how do you distinguish yourself from others so that you score the ideal remote job? 
How do I set up my resume specifically for a remote position? 
Start with a powerful summary that names your passion, work experience and relevant skills for remote work. Then incorporate concrete examples from your work experience, including roles, periods and specific results achieved remotely with tools such as Slack, Zoom or Trello.  This is how you demonstrate your remote success stories in a targeted way.
What technical and soft skills should I include on my remote resume? 
Mention digital skills such as Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Slack, Trello, Asana and Google Workspace. Add essential soft skills such as independence, time management, strategic insight and problem solving.  Use concrete examples to demonstrate how you have applied these skills in practice.
What do I show in work experience section for remote jobs? 
For each role, list the organization, time period and position in one line. Describe specifically how you worked remotely, what tools you used and what results you achieved. Think productivity gains or project results . This is how you make your contribution to remote teams transparent.
How important is a profile on a remote resume? 
A profile under your personalia is crucial. Briefly describe yourself as a professional, your motivation and remote experience. Avoid woolly language and emphasize core qualities for virtual collaboration. This will make it immediately clear that you are suited for remote working. 
Why do you add personality to a remote resume? 
Remote teams lack office interaction, so culture and personality are also important. Add hobbies, languages, volunteer work or interests. That makes you human, engaged and culturally rich. Recruiters will see your contribution to an inclusive, virtual team culture this way .
