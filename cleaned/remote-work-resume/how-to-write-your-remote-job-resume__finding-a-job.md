## Why should you include remote work experience on your resume?
If you have remote work experience, it's important to tailor your resume to let your prospective employer know you're well qualified for their remote position. Just as any resume highlights your skills and qualifications, your remote job resume needs to reflect your ability to work from home if this is the type of job you're applying for.Related: [Q&A: What Is Remote Work?](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/finding-a-job/remote-work)

## How to write your remote job resume
Showcasing your experience with remote work and your transferable skills can help you stay ahead of the competition. Here are some ways to add work-from-home (WFH) experience to your resume:
  1. Include your remote work experience in your [resume summary](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/resumes-cover-letters/writing-a-resume-summary-with-examples).
  2. Mention your remote work experience in the location section.
  3. Create [an additional section](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/resumes-cover-letters/best-resume-templates) for your remote work experience.
  4. Add it to your work history descriptions.
  5. Incorporate it into your skills section.


### 1. Include your remote work experience in your resume summary
If you have a summary of your qualifications on your resume, consider detailing your remote work experience there. Since this section is at the top of your resume, hiring managers will know you can work remotely right away. This section is typically a bulleted list and provides an overview of your experience and how it relates to the job. Include details about your history of working remotely and how that's beneficial to the company.Here is an example of how to include it in your resume summary:
  * 10 years of experience working as a [graphic designer](https://www.indeed.com/jobs?q=graphic%20designer&l=remote&from=searchOnHP%2Cwhatautocomplete)
  * Well-versed in working with a variety of clients and deadlines
  * Managed a team of skilled designers and led a company rebranding
  * Maintained a work-from-home role for four years

Related: [How To Work From Home](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/finding-a-job/how-can-i-work-from-home)
### 2. Mention your remote work experience in the location section
You can also mention your remote work history when you list your previous jobs in the work experience section of your resume. Do this by writing a brief sentence that tells hiring managers you performed a certain job remotely in the job description, by omitting a company's city and state and adding a sentence that says the job was remote or by writing "remote work" instead of the city and state altogether. Here is an example to consider:Blue Mountain Agency  
Remote Work  
October 2017 - January 2020
### 3. Create an additional section for your remote work experience
If you have enough remote work experience, consider creating a new section on your resume devoted to this work style. This is great for showing remote employers that you have plenty of experience in this area and that you're able to successfully perform the job they're hiring.Related: [How to Write Your Resume Employment History (With Examples)](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/resumes-cover-letters/resume-employment-history)
### 4. Add it to your work history descriptions
Another way to include your remote work history is to mention it as you detail your previous job responsibilities. Just as you'd mention what you did in your previous roles, add a sentence that states you performed this job off-site. Here is an example:Answered customer calls, questions and concerns from my home office with high-speed internet access.
### 5. Incorporate it into your skills section
Since working remotely is a skill, you can include your experience with it in the [skills section](https://ca.indeed.com/career-advice/resumes-cover-letters/resume-skills) of your resume. It's also important to consider how your other skills can benefit a remote atmosphere. For example, if you have strong communication skills, consider mentioning your ability to stay connected through video calls, phone calls or email, as these are common ways WFH employees communicate with their team.Related: [How to Search for Remote Work on Indeed](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/finding-a-job/find-remote-work-on-indeed)
## Tips for writing your remote job resume
Along with knowing how to create a WFH resume, it's also important to consider ways to help your resume stand out from other applicants'. Here are some tips to help you create an impressive resume and attain worthwhile job opportunities:
### Tailor it to a specific job
When you write a resume, consider the job you're applying for. If you're applying for a remote job, in particular, make sure you've determined how the company operates remotely. Also, you need to review the job description and use only your relevant work history and skills on your resume. Then, create a resume that fits the company's needs while showing hiring managers how you can positively add to their remote workforce.Related: [How To Tailor a Resume to a Job (With Video)](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/resumes-cover-letters/how-to-tailor-a-resume-to-a-job)
### Highlight your transferable skills
If you're seeking opportunities in a new field, highlight the skills and experience you have that can translate to another position. For example, if you have strong time management skills, consider explaining how you can use this skill in a variety of industries and how it's beneficial to a remote position.Related: [10 Best Skills To Put On Your Resume (With Examples and FAQ)](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/resumes-cover-letters/best-resume-skills)

Along with helping you with your everyday job-seeking needs, Indeed has a way for you to alert employers that you're looking for work immediately.
