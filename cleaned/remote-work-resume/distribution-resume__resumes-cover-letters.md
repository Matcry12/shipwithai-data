## What is a distribution resume?
A distribution resume is a document that job applicants submit when applying for a position. It shares background information about the applicant with the employer, such as education and work experience. A distribution resume is a specialized resume to apply for jobs in distribution, such as a warehouse manager or a machine operator. Distribution is a stage in sales where associates sort, prepare and ship products to customers or to retail stores.Related: [How To Start a Distribution Business](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/finding-a-job/how-to-start-a-distribution-business)

## Why is a distribution resume important?
Writing an impactful distribution resume is a key factor in getting a job in distribution. Employers are more likely to consider applicants with a resume and invite them for an interview. A resume is also important to achieve the following:
### Demonstrates your job qualifications
An effective resume highlights your most relevant skills for potential employers. It also outlines your work experience and educational background to help show that you have the qualifications that the job position requires. It's important to be concise with the information you share. Try to include the most recent work experience or work experience that most closely relates to the job to which you are applying. Employers need to be able to read your resume quickly and determine if you're a good candidate.
### Shares how you can benefit the company
A resume can share the value you can bring to the company and how your experience and skill level can benefit them. A great place to inform recruiters of how you can benefit the company is in a resume objective. This is a statement that summarizes your resume and can include your career goals, which may align with company goals. For example, you may say you're looking to advance your career in distribution from warehouse associate to team lead by collaborating and increasing the efficiency at which you prepare orders for shipment.
### Gets attention of recruiters
A job application can gain more attention from recruiters when a resume accompanies it. An attractive resume that is properly formatted, free of errors and includes all the necessary information can encourage recruiters to select you for interviews. Adding color to your resume and bold titles can help, but it's important to focus on the information the resume contains, more so than its design.Related: [15 Resume Revision Tips To Get You Noticed by Employers](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/resumes-cover-letters/resume-revision)
## 5 jobs in distribution
Here are examples of jobs you may write a distribution resume for:
  * Distribution associate: A distribution associate often works in a warehouse and handles warehouse operations, such as packing items for shipment, receiving shipments and organizing products. They also manage customer orders and the inventory within the warehouse.
  * Materials handler: A materials handler works in a warehouse for manufacturing and they're responsible for managing inventory and moving products for shipping or storage. Material handlers help to fill customer orders, locate products and ensure that the product gets shipped.
  * Inventory control specialist: Inventory control specialists can work in a variety of industries, and their primary role is to maintain inventory and manage the stock of products. They may restock shelves, order products and keep records of inventory.
  * Machine operator: Machine operators are professionals who use heavy machinery, such as a forklift, in warehouses. They inspect the machinery they use and order maintenance when needed. Machine operators also help to move or produce products with machines.
  * Distribution center manager: Distribution center managers are responsible for the operations of a warehouse, manufacturing, building or distribution center. They manage employees, train new hires and assign job tasks.


## How to write a distribution resume
Follow these steps to help write a distribution resume:
### 1. Organize necessary information
It's helpful to gather all the information you need for a resume before you begin writing. This can help you organize your thoughts. The personal information you need to have for your resume includes:
  * Current address and contact information
  * Name of schools or college you attended
  * Companies and dates of employment from your work history
  * Professional skills

Related: [What a Resume Is and What To Include](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/resumes-cover-letters/what-is-a-resume)
### 2. Write a resume header
A resume header is on the top of your resume and includes your name, address and contact information. Often, headers are a good place to add color to your resume to help employers take notice. Ensure that your address is current and that you include a phone number and email address so that employers are able to contact you easily.Read more: [How To Write Resume Headers](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/resumes-cover-letters/resume-headers)
### 3. Include a resume objective
Include a resume objective under the header at the top of your resume. This is a summary of your resume and helps employers recognize that you're a candidate with qualifications who can add value to the company. In a distribution resume objective, it's effective to mention the position to which you're applying and the skills you have to help qualify you for the job. For example, if you're applying to be a distribution center manager, consider including skills such as strategic planning and creating budgets.
### 4. List your education
Many distribution careers require a high school diploma only and offer on-the-job training, but senior-level positions such as a distribution center manager, require at least an associate degree. Include your highest level of education on your resume and the dates that you graduated.Related: [How To Include Your High School Education on a Resume](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/resumes-cover-letters/high-school-education-on-resume)
### 5. Describe recent or relevant work experience
Your work experience often is the best indicator of your job qualifications. Try to include your most recent job experience. It's important to keep your resume concise so you may limit work experience to three jobs. If you have held many jobs, consider which are the most relevant to the position. Check the job description to see if any previous job duties relate to the responsibilities of the open position.
### 6. Create a list of your skills
Listing your skill set is another way to demonstrate your qualifications to employers. Typically, it's a good practice to include four or five of the skills which you can perform at an expert level. Examples of skills for distribution careers include:
  * Customer service
  * Communication
  * Teamwork
  * Computer literacy
  * Operating machinery

Related: [Soft Skills In The Workplace](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/career-development/soft-skills-in-the-workplace)
### 7. Proofread your resume
Ensuring the accuracy of all information you share on your resume and using correct spelling and grammar shows you are professional and serious about the job to which you apply. A proofread resume can help make a good first impression with employers and increase your opportunity to receive a job interview.Related: [27 Proofreading Tips That Will Improve Your Resume](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/resumes-cover-letters/proofreading-tips-that-will-improve-your-resume)

## Tips to help write a resume for distribution jobs
Consider these tips when creating a distribution resume:
### Personalize for each job application
Read through the job description and look for ways you can personalize your resume for the open position. A good way to do this is to list skills you have that the employer mentions in the job description. You can also state the position you are applying to and the name of the company on your resume in the objective statement. Personalizing a resume can show your genuine interest in a position.
### Be unique
Find ways to be unique and give yourself a possible advantage on other candidates seeking an open position. Consider special skills such as speaking another language, certification in CPR or a technical skill which you can apply to the job.
### Use keywords
Using keywords in your resume from the job description helps to get it to recruiters. Often, companies use an applicant tracking system that identifies the best candidates and helps sort resumes. One way an applicant tracking system sorts resumes is by finding keywords and marking resumes that use them as qualified job candidates.
