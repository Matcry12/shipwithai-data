## How to write Technology Executive Resume
Technology Executive role is responsible for organizational, database, recruiting, software, training, printing, compensation, analysis, reporting, design.   
To write great resume for technology executive job, your resume must include: 
  * Your contact information
  * Work experience
  * Education
  * Skill listing


### Contact Information For Technology Executive Resume
The section contact information is important in your technology executive resume. The recruiter has to be able to contact you ASAP if they like to offer you the job. This is why you need to provide your: 
  * First and last name
  * Email
  * Telephone number


### Work Experience in Your Technology Executive Resume
The section work experience is an essential part of your technology executive resume. It’s the one thing the recruiter really cares about and pays the most attention to.   
This section, however, is not just a list of your previous technology executive responsibilities. It's meant to present you as a wholesome candidate by showcasing your relevant accomplishments and should be tailored specifically to the particular technology executive position you're applying to. The work experience section should be the detailed summary of your latest 3 or 4 positions. 
Representative Technology Executive resume experience can include:
  * In excess of 10 years demonstrated experience at a management level, being able to articulate
  * Taffing professional experience required
  * Experience selling Managed Print Services
  * Management or equivalent experience required
  * Developing and handling Software Development Standards and Practices and associated governance for the entire IT group
  * Working within a “life sciences” organization to grow the Teradata global footprint


### Education on a Technology Executive Resume
Make sure to make education a priority on your technology executive resume. If you’ve been working for a few years and have a few solid positions to show, put your education after your technology executive experience. For example, if you have a Ph.D in Neuroscience and a Master's in the same sphere, just list your Ph.D. Besides the doctorate, Master’s degrees go next, followed by Bachelor’s and finally, Associate’s degree. 
Additional details to include: 
  * School you graduated from
  * Major/ minor
  * Year of graduation
  * Location of school


These are the four additional pieces of information you should mention when listing your education on your resume.
