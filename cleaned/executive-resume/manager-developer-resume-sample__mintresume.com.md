## How to write Manager, Developer Resume

Manager, Developer role is responsible for programming, translation, training, integration, database, security, java, architecture, design, research.

To write great resume for manager, developer job, your resume must include:

- Your contact information
- Work experience
- Education
- Skill listing

### Contact Information For Manager, Developer Resume

The section contact information is important in your manager, developer resume. The recruiter has to be able to contact you ASAP if they like to offer you the job. This is why you need to provide your:

- First and last name
- Telephone number

### Work Experience in Your Manager, Developer Resume

The section work experience is an essential part of your manager, developer resume. It's the one thing the recruiter really cares about and pays the most attention to.

This section, however, is not just a list of your previous manager, developer responsibilities. It's meant to present you as a wholesome candidate by showcasing your relevant accomplishments and should be tailored specifically to the particular manager, developer position you're applying to.
The work experience section should be the detailed summary of your latest 3 or 4 positions.

Representative Manager, Developer resume experience can include:

- Good experience with java builds automation
- At least three (3) years of experience with using FileNet, FileNet APIs, Case Manager APIs and IBM Content Navigator required
- Build and support solutions on the Adobe Experience Manager (AEM) platform, including Java, RESTful web services, Javascript, jQuery, AJAX, JSON, HTML, CSS
- Good understanding of REST API integrations
- Experience in Salesforce.com platform
- Java with HTML web application development experience, RESTful web services, CSS

### Education on a Manager, Developer Resume

Make sure to make education a priority on your manager, developer resume. If you've been working for a few years and have a few solid positions to show, put your education after your manager, developer experience. For example, if you have a Ph.D in Neuroscience and a Master's in the same sphere, just list your Ph.D. Besides the doctorate, Master's degrees go next, followed by Bachelor's and finally, Associate's degree.

Additional details to include:

- School you graduated from
- Major/ minor
- Year of graduation
- Location of school

These are the four additional pieces of information you should mention when listing your education on your resume.

### Professional Skills in Manager, Developer Resume

When listing skills on your manager, developer resume, remember always to be honest about your level of ability. Include the Skills section after experience.

Present the most important skills in your resume, there's a list of typical manager, developer skills:

- Strong understanding of concepts as: versioning, branching, defect tracking and release management
- Good working knowledge in Unix/Linux shell scripting; able to navigate, work, install, and use shell scripting
- At least five (5) years of experience developing JavaScript code using Dojo, and integrating with IBM Case Manager required
- Strong understanding of relational databases (including tables, views, indexes, tablespaces etc.)
- Experience with any versioning, defect tracking and release management tool as: Jira, ClearQuest, SNOW, etc
- Experience developing solutions with IBM Case Manager 5.2.x
