# How To Write A Software Development Manager Resume

Updated 3 December 2025

A software development manager oversees a team of developers and ensures they performs tasks punctually, affordably and satisfactorily for the client. They require extensive education, training and experience in both software development and management. Learning more about how to create an effective resume for this role can help increase your chances of getting hired. In this article, we review how to create a strong software development manager resume and provide a resume template and three resume samples for diverse employment scenarios.Please note that none of the companies, institutions or organisations mentioned in this article are associated with Indeed.

## How To Write A Software Development Manager Resume?

To write an effective software development manager resume, follow these steps:### 1. Start with your contact information

Provide your name, location, email address and phone number at the top of your resume. Because hiring managers use this information to contact you, double-check it to make sure it is correct. Also mention any relevant degrees or certifications that can gain the recruiter's attention.### 2. Write a professional summary

For your resume objective, clearly state your experience and skills in managing software development teams, along with your ability to lead the development of software projects from concept to delivery. Highlight any relevant technical skills and experience you may have in specific programming languages or development methodologies. Use this section to address any discrepancies on your resume, such as a career shift or a gap in experience.Related: How To Write A Resume Summary (With 30 Examples)### 3. Include relevant professional experience

Organise your experience list reverse-chronologically with necessary information such as job title, company name, duration and location. Make sure the majority of your bullet points discuss the precise technical knowledge, abilities and software you have used as a software development manager. Quantify your accomplishments with numbers or statistics wherever you can. As this is a senior role, be selective with the experience statements and provide more information about positions where you participated in software development or managing teams. Use keywords from the job description as a guide to highlight your relevant competencies and expertise.Related: How To Write Work Experience On Your CV### 4. Mention hard and soft skills

Hard skills are technical knowledge acquired through training. Soft skills are personal attributes that help you work effectively in a team and manage others. Some examples of soft skills relevant to the role include leadership, communication, time management, strategic thinking, mentorship and problem-solving. Hard skills for the role can include experience with or understanding of software development processes and methodologies, project management tools, specific programming languages and technologies used by the team.Related: How To List Your Skills On A Resume (With Examples)### 5. Provide your educational qualifications

Highlight your relevant credentials, including any certifications you may have that can help you as a software development manager. Usually, employers request a bachelor's or master's degree in computer science, computer engineering or a related field when hiring for this role. Even though it is not mandatory, certain management qualifications, such as an MBA, can be highly impressive for such a position. For each educational entry, list the degree or diploma name, the institution you attended and its location. For certifications, list the name, the issuing organisation and the date you received it or its expiry.Related: How To List Education On Your Resume (With Examples)Related jobs on Indeed
