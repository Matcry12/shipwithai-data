##  Meet Sarah, returning to work after some time off 
After taking some time away from work, Sarah had decided she was ready to return. Having worked in finance for many years, she already had qualifications and experience that are desirable to many employers.
But she wasn't sure how to explain the gap in her CV and was worried this would put off recruiters. After attending an appointment with a careers adviser, Sarah applied for the Women Returners Programme to build her confidence. Now, Sarah is confident her CV highlights the effort she's made to refamiliarise herself with the labour market.

### Work experience
Before your time out, you'll have spent some time working and building a career. Therefore, you'll have lots of experience to chat about. However, keep this section relevant to the role you're applying for. 
Employers want to hear about your previous jobs or volunteering positions, who you worked for and for how long. A short overview of your duties in each role is a great way to show how much responsibility you had too.
