# [ Martin Poduška  Editor In Chief / Career Writer ](https://www.kickresume.com/en/help-center/authors/martin-poduska/)
Martin is the most senior resume expert and career advice writer collaborating with Kickresume. In his nine years with Kickresume, he has written hundreds of in-depth, painstakingly researched resume advice articles and, as chief editor, he has also edited and revised every single article on this website. Tens of thousands of job seekers read Martin’s resume advice every month, often on outlets like AdWeek, Fast Company, and others. He holds a degree in English from the [University of St Andrews](https://www.st-andrews.ac.uk) and a degree in Comparative Literature from the [University of Amsterdam](https://www.uva.nl).
