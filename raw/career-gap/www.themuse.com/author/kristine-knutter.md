# Kristine Knutter
Kristine Knutter is a communication and career coach who works with 200+ job seekers and physicians annually to express themselves clearly, confidently, and persuasively to build trust and credibility with employers and achieve their career goals. She’s the host of the popular [_Express to Impress Podcast_](https://www.express-to-impress.com/podcast/) and instructor of a five-hour [online course for job seekers](https://www.express-to-impress.com/courses/). TEFL-certified, she specializes in ESL job seekers and physicians and has a vast wealth of experience and knowledge regarding cultural differences that impact communication in the workplace.
