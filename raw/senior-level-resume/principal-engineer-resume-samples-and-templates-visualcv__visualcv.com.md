- List your top Principal Engineer skills in a separate skills section.

## How to Write a Principal Engineer Resume Experience Section?

Here’s how you can write a job-winning Principal Engineer resume experience section:

- Use bullets instead of paragraphs to explain your Principal Engineer work experience.
- While describing your work experience, focus on highlighting what you did and the impact you made (you can use numbers to describe your success as a Principal Engineer).
- Use action verbs in your bullet points.

## How Long Should my Principal Engineer Resume be?

Your Principal Engineer resume length should be less than one or two pages maximum. Unless you have more than 25 years of experience, any resume that’s more than two pages would appear to be too long and risk getting rejected.

On average, for Principal Engineer, we see most resumes have a length of 2. And, that’s why we advise you to keep the resume length appropriate to not get rejected.
