## Formatting the layout of your tech lead resume: design, length, and more 
When it comes to the [format of your tech lead resume ](https://enhancv.com/blog/resume-format/), you've plenty of opportunities to get creative. But, as a general rule of thumb, there are four simple steps you could integrate into your resume layout.
  * If you have plenty of experience, you'd like to showcase, invest in the [reverse-chronological resume format](https://enhancv.com/blog/reverse-chronological-resume/). This format focuses on your latest experience items and skills you've learned during your relevant (and recent) jobs. 
  * Don't go over the two-page limit, when creating your professional tech lead resume. Curate within it mainly experience and skills that are relevant to the job. 
  * Make sure your tech lead resume header includes all of your valid contact information. You could also opt to display your professional portfolio or LinkedIn profile. 
  * Submit or send out your tech lead resume as a PDF, so you won't lose its layout and design. 


Align your resume with the market’s standards – [Canadian resumes may have unique layout guidelines](https://enhancv.com/blog/canadian-resume-format/).

## Strategies for crafting your tech lead resume experience section
When detailing your tech lead [resume experience](https://enhancv.com/blog/resume-work-experience/), it's essential to pair responsibilities with tangible achievements.
Consider including:
  * Key responsibilities, emphasizing their significance to your role, team, or organization.
  * Experiences that have fostered your technical acumen or professional growth.
  * Metrics that underscore your contributions and successes.
  * Challenges you've addressed and the solutions you've implemented.
  * Strategies you've devised and their measurable impact on growth.

### Quantifying impact on your resume
&lt;ul&gt;
* Include the size of the team you managed. This shows your capacity to handle responsibility and provide leadership.
* List the number of projects you successfully led from start to finish, demonstrating project management skills and reliability.
* Describe the scale of the systems or software you have worked on in terms of users or transactions handled, illustrating your ability to work on high-stakes, large-scale projects.
* State any quantifiable improvements made, such as system performance enhancements or reduced error rates, demonstrating a results-oriented approach.
* Specify the percentage by which you helped reduce costs or time consumption by streamlining processes or implementing new technologies, showing your efficiency and ability to add value.
* Provide the number of issues or bugs resolved under your supervision, indicating problem-solving skills and attention to detail.
* Mention the amount of revenue generated or profits increased due to your technological contributions, showing your impact on business success.
* Detail the number of technical trainings or workshops conducted or attended, highlighting your commitment to continuous learning and knowledge sharing.

  * In your objective, outline your career aspirations and how they align with the company's goals.
  * Consider a functional or hybrid resume format, focusing on skills over chronological experience.

## Essential tech lead resume skills
When recruiters review tech lead resumes, they're looking for a mix of technical know-how and personal attributes.
[Technical skills](https://enhancv.com/blog/hard-skills-on-resume/) demonstrate your proficiency in specific tools or tasks. They indicate if you're ready to jump into the role or if you'll need extensive training.
On the other hand, [soft skills](https://enhancv.com/blog/soft-skills-on-resume/) reflect your interpersonal abilities. They show how you'll fit into a team or company culture.
To effectively present these skills on your resume:
  * Design a skills section that highlights both your technical and interpersonal strengths.
  * Provide examples where you've applied these skills, such as projects or tasks.
  * For soft skills, describe situations where they've been crucial to your success.
  * Use metrics, like improved efficiency or positive feedback, to validate your skills.

## Highlighting education and certification on your tech lead resume
Your [education section](https://enhancv.com/blog/resume-education-section/) is a testament to your foundational knowledge and expertise.
Consider:
  * Detailing your academic qualifications, including the institution and duration.
  * If you're still studying, mention your anticipated graduation date.
  * Omit degrees that aren't pertinent to the job.
  * Highlight academic experiences that underscore significant milestones.


For tech lead roles, relevant education and [certifications](https://enhancv.com/blog/certifications-on-resume/) can set you apart.
To effectively showcase your qualifications:
  * List all pertinent degrees and certifications in line with the job requirements.
  * Include additional certifications if they bolster your application.
  * Provide concise details: certification name, institution, and dates.
  * If you're pursuing a relevant certification, indicate your expected completion date.


Your education and certification sections validate both your foundational and advanced knowledge in the industry.

## Summary or objective: making your tech lead resume shine
Start your resume with a strong summary or objective to grab the recruiter's attention.
  * Use a [resume objective](https://enhancv.com/blog/resume-objective/) if you're newer to the field. Share your career dreams and strengths.
  * Opt for a [resume summary](https://enhancv.com/blog/resume-summary/) if you have more experience. Highlight up to five of your top achievements.


Tailor your summary or objective for each job. Think about what the recruiter wants to see.
