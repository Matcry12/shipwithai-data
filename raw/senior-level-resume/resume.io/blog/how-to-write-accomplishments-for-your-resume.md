# How to Write Accomplishments for Your Resume
Last Updated: April 22, 2026
![How to Write Accomplishments for Your Resume](https://s3.resume.io/cdn-cgi/image/width=420,height=368,fit=cover,quality=70,format=auto/uploads/blog_post/featured_image/2829/how-to-write-accomplishments-for-your-resum.jpg)
While a resume should reflect the highlights of your [career](https://resume.io/blog/how-to-grow-your-career), describing accomplishments takes more than listing what you did. 
[Strong examples](https://resume.io/resume-examples) help employers picture how you would perform in their organization, making your value clear from the start.
## List of accomplishments for a resume
[Resume](https://resume.io) accomplishments are results that show how your work created measurable impact.
All of them involve taking action that led to a positive result for your employer. 
When choosing which accomplishments to include, focus on examples that show how your work made an impact and how that impact relates to what you will be expected to do in your next role.
Here is a list of accomplishments for a resume that you can use as inspiration or a starting point:
  * Increased sales or profit margins
  * Reduced costs or improved the bottom line
  * Improved productivity or time efficiency
  * Solved problems through new ideas or process improvements
  * Introduced innovations that led to measurable change
  * Improved workflows, systems, or procedures
  * Earned awards or recognition (with context)
  * Achieved promotion or expanded responsibilities
  * Supported the development or performance of others
  * Delivered strong service to customers or clients

| **Cost savings**  | Efficiency and financial impact  | _Reduced operating costs by 15% by improving workflows_  |

### What are 3 accomplishments you can include on a resume?
Three common types of accomplishments to include are:
  * **Increasing revenue or performance** (for example, improving sales or output)
  * **Reducing costs or improving efficiency** (such as saving time or resources)
  * **Earning recognition or responsibility**(such as promotions, awards, or leadership roles)


In all of these cases, the accomplishment shows the difference you made.
## How to list accomplishments in your resume
To list accomplishments effectively, focus on results, not responsibilities. 
Strong accomplishment statements clearly show what you did, how you did it, and what impact it had. 
This helps hiring managers quickly understand what you can bring.
### Step 1: Start with a clear action
Begin each accomplishment with a strong action verb that shows what you did. 
This immediately highlights your role and makes your experience easier to scan.
Be specific about your contribution, especially if you worked as part of a team.
### Step 2: Describe the task and context
Briefly explain what you worked on or were responsible for. 
Keep this concise and focused on relevant details that help explain the achievement.
### Step 3: Show the result with numbers
The result is the most important part. 
Show what changed because of your work and include numbers where possible.
You can [quantify your accomplishments](https://resume.io/blog/quantify-your-resume-bullets) using:
  * **Timeframe** (how quickly results were achieved)
  * **Scale**(size of the impact)
  * **Range**(consistency or volume of results)


**Top tip:** When writing your resume, start by listing your responsibilities, then rewrite each one as a result-focused accomplishment.
### Example of turning a task into an accomplishment
  * **Before:** _Managed social media accounts_
  * **After:** _Increased social media engagement by 45% over six months through a targeted content strategy_


### Using the STAR method
You can also structure accomplishments using the [STAR method](https://resume.io/blog/star-method-resume) (Situation, Task, Action, Result). 
On your resume, focus mainly on the action and result, keeping the context brief.
Use [Resume.io’s resume builder](https://resume.io/app/create-resume/templates) to quickly test different accomplishment statements and refine your [results-focused bullet points](https://resume.io/blog/quantify-your-resume-bullets).

## What are examples of STAR statements for resume achievements?
STAR statements help you turn responsibilities into clear, results-focused accomplishments by showing the situation, task, action, and result. 
On a resume, you should keep the situation and task brief, and focus on the action and measurable result.
### Example 1
**Situation/Task:** Managed social media accounts for a growing brand  
**Action:** Developed and implemented a targeted content strategy  
**Result:** Increased engagement by 45% over six months
**Resume version:** _Increased social media engagement by 45% in six months by implementing a targeted content strategy._
### Example 2
**Situation/Task:** Responsible for onboarding new employees  
**Action:** Created a structured training program  
**Result:** Reduced onboarding time by 30%
**Resume version:** _Reduced onboarding time by 30% by introducing a structured employee training program._
### Example 3
**Situation/Task:** Handled customer complaints  
**Action:** Improved response process and follow-up system  
**Result:** Increased customer satisfaction scores by 20%
**Resume version:**_Improved customer satisfaction scores by 20% by redesigning complaint response and follow-up processes._
## Where to list accomplishments in a resume
You should include accomplishments in the sections of your resume where employers expect to see proof of your impact. 
The most important places are your [work experience](https://resume.io/blog/writing-work-experience-on-a-resume) and your [resume summary](https://resume.io/blog/resume-summary), where achievements help demonstrate your value quickly.
### 1. Work experience section
Most resumes include a work experience section with several [bullet points](https://resume.io/blog/resume-bullets) for each role. 
This is the main place to highlight your accomplishments.
You can include achievements within your bullet points or list them separately if there are several great examples. 
Focus on measurable results that show how you contributed in each role.
### 2. Resume summary section
Your resume summary is one of the first things a hiring manager reads, so it is a strong place to highlight one or two key accomplishments.
Keep them concise and relevant to the role. 
A well-chosen achievement at the top of your resume can immediately show your worth and encourage the reader to continue.
### 3. Optional: Awards and highlights
If [awards](https://resume.io/blog/how-to-list-awards-on-a-resume-with-examples) or recognitions are important in your industry, you can include them either in your work experience or as a separate section.
In most cases, it is more effective to include them alongside the relevant role so they are supported by context.

## What are the best practices for listing sports achievements on a resume?
Sports achievements can be valuable on a resume if they demonstrate skills like [teamwork](https://resume.io/blog/teamwork-skills), [leadership](https://resume.io/blog/leadership-skills), discipline, or performance under pressure. 
The key is to present them in a way that connects to the job you are applying for.
### 1. Focus on transferable skills
Highlight what the achievement shows about you, not just what happened. 
For example, team captain roles show leadership, while competitive results can demonstrate discipline and consistency.
### 2. Keep them relevant
Include sports achievements if they add value to your application. 
This is especially useful if you are early in your career, have limited work experience, or the role involves teamwork or performance.
Avoid listing older or less relevant achievements if you already have strong professional experience.
### 3. Add context and results
Where possible, treat sports achievements like professional accomplishments. 
Include measurable details such as rankings, wins, or improvements.
#### For example:
  * _Led college team to regional championship win_
  * _Ranked top 5 in state competition over two seasons_
  * _Trained 15 hours per week while maintaining academic performance_


### Where do I include these achievements?
You can include sports achievements in:
  * [Education section](https://resume.io/blog/list-education-on-your-resume) (if linked to school or university)
  * Work experience (if coaching, training, or leadership is involved)


## Key takeaways
Strong resume accomplishments show the impact of your work, not just your responsibilities. 
Focus on understandable, measurable results that help employers understand your value quickly.
  * Use a simple structure: action, task, and result
  * Start with [strong action verbs](https://resume.io/blog/300-powerful-resume-action-verbs) to highlight your role
  * Quantify your achievements with numbers, percentages, or timeframes
  * Focus on results that are relevant to the job you are applying for
  * Include accomplishments in your work experience and summary sections
  * Avoid vague statements that do not show measurable impact

## FAQs for resume accomplishments 
Short answers to common questions about choosing and writing strong resume accomplishments.
### What are good accomplishments to put on a resume?
Good accomplishments are results that show how your work made a measurable impact. 
This often includes increasing revenue, improving efficiency, solving problems, or earning recognition such as [promotions](https://resume.io/blog/how-to-show-a-promotion-on-your-resume) or awards. 
Focus on achievements that are relevant to the role and supported by clear results.
### What are some amazing accomplishments?
Amazing accomplishments are those that show exceptional impact, scale, or progression. 
Examples include significantly exceeding targets, leading major projects, saving substantial costs, or being promoted quickly. 
The strongest examples combine clear action with impressive, measurable results.
