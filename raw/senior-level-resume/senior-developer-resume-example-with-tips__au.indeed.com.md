## What to include in a senior developer resume

When compiling your senior developer resume, it's crucial to emphasise elements that best display your technical abilities, professional accomplishments and dedication to driving business success. Here are five key sections that your senior developer resume needs to cover.### Contact details

Your full name, location, phone number and a professional email address. Ensure that your details are up-to-date and correct so recruiters can easily get in touch with you.### Professional summary

A brief overview of your career, highlighting specific skills, years of experience and major accomplishments. Align the summary with the job requirements of the position you're applying for.### Work experience

This section comprises details of your professional history in reverse chronological order. Include job title, company, dates of employment and concisely list your responsibilities and achievements in each role. Try to quantify your achievements where possible if you have relevant examples, to maximise their impact.### Technical skills

List your technical skills here. This might include programming languages, systems, tools, methodologies, and other skills that you are proficient in. Also keep this section tailored to the job description.### Certifications

Include any specialised technical certificates that you've obtained. Your senior developer resume is your first point of contact with a potential employer. Therefore, it is essential to prepare an impactful and convincing document. Here are three tips to help you stand out.### Customise to the job description

Each job description is unique. It helps if you customise your resume to match the skills and experience desired for a specific role. Using relevant keywords from the job description can also enhance your visibility if the employer uses automated screening systems.### Highlight problem-solving abilities

As a senior developer, your ability to solve problems efficiently is highly sought-after. Highlight instances from your previous roles where you used your technical prowess to solve challenging issues.### Show management and leadership experience

If you've managed teams or projects, make sure you include this in your resume. Demonstrating your leadership and team management skills can give you an edge over other candidates, as this indicates your readiness for senior-level responsibilities.Related: Writing a Resume with No Experience## Frequently asked questions

The following are some frequently asked questions about writing a senior developer resume.### How long does a senior developer resume need to be?

Ideally, a senior developer resume is one to two pages long. This length allows you to comprehensively detail your work experience, technical skills, certifications and educational background without overwhelming potential employers. However, always focus on quality over quantity, ensuring that every bit of information you provide supports and adds value to your application.### How do I list programming languages on a resume?

As a senior developer, your proficiency in various programming languages can set you apart from other candidates. You can incorporate them under the 'Skills' section, listing them individually, such as Python, Java, C#, and others. Alternatively, you can include them in your professional experience descriptions, indicating how you employed them in particular roles or projects.### How do I show promotions on a resume?

When you've been promoted within a company, it's important to clearly indicate this progression on your resume. List the company once, and then list each position you held in reverse chronological order underneath, along with the respective dates. This approach illustrates your career growth, dedication and recognition in a clear and concise manner.### Should I include a cover letter with my resume?

Though not always mandatory, it's a good idea to include a cover letter with your senior developer resume.
