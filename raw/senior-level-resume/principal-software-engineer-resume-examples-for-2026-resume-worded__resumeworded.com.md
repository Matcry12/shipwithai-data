This position is the highest ranking software engineer position and there is usually only one principal software engineer in a company. In this position, you will oversee the entire software engineering department and so may find yourself spending more time managing and leading teams and projects, rather than doing the software engineering yourself. Regardless, recruiters will need to see that you are an expert at software engineering and that you have had a long and successful career.

We spoke with hiring managers and recruiters at top tech companies like Google, Microsoft, and Amazon to gather their best tips for creating a standout principal software engineer resume. They emphasized the importance of showcasing your technical expertise, leadership skills, and impact on projects and teams. Use the following tips to make your resume shine and increase your chances of landing your dream job.

As a Principal Software Engineer, your resume summary is an optional section that can help provide additional context and highlight your most relevant qualifications. While your resume itself is already a summary of your experience, a well-crafted summary statement can be particularly useful if you're a career changer or have a diverse background that may not immediately align with the Principal Software Engineer role you're targeting.

However, it's important to avoid using an objective statement, as these are outdated and focus on your own goals rather than how you can contribute to the company. Instead, use your summary to showcase your unique value proposition and the key skills and experiences that make you a strong fit for the position.

The work experience section is the heart of your resume. It's where you prove to employers that you have the right experience for the principal software engineer position. Let's cover the key steps to writing an effective work experience section that will impress hiring managers and help you land interviews.

The education section of your principal software engineer resume should be concise and highlight your most relevant academic achievements. Position this section strategically based on your career level and the importance of your educational background to the role.

The skills section on your principal software engineer resume is critical to get right. Hiring managers and recruiters use this section to quickly assess if you have the technical chops for the role. It's also important to pass the Applicant Tracking System (ATS) filters that screen out candidates missing key skills.
