## What Do Hiring Managers Look for in a Senior Engineering Manager Resume
  1. Strong leadership skills to inspire and guide engineering teams.
  2. Technical expertise to provide informed decision-making and guidance.
  3. Strategic thinking to align engineering goals with business objectives.
  4. Excellent communication skills for effective collaboration and stakeholder management.
  5. Proficiency in project management to ensure successful project delivery.

## How to Write Your Senior Engineering Manager Resume Header?
Write the perfect Senior Engineering Manager resume header by:
  * Adding your full name at the top of the header. 
  * Add a photo to your resume if you are applying for jobs outside of the US. For applying to jobs within the US, avoid adding photo to your resume header. 
  * Add your current Senior Engineering position to the header to show relevance. 
  * Add your current city, your phone number and a professional email address. 
  * Finally, add a link to your portfolio to the Senior Engineering Manager resume header. If there’s no portfolio link to add, consider adding a link to your LinkedIn profile instead. 


### Bad Senior Engineering Manager Resume Example - Header Section
Deon 167 Hamilton Drive Phillipsburg, NJ 08865 Marital Status: Married, email: cooldude2022@gmail.com
### Good Senior Engineering Manager Resume Example - Header Section
Deon Nguyen, Phillipsburg, NJ, Phone number: +1-555-555-5555, Link: linkedin/in/johndoe
Make sure to add a professional looking email address while writing your resume header. Let’s assume your name is John Doe - here is a formula you can use to create email addresses:
  * firstnamelastname@email.com - johndoe@email.com 
  * firstname.lastname@email.com - john.doe@email.com 
  * lastname.firstname@email.com - doe.john@email.com 
  * f.lastname@email.com - j.doe@email.com 
  * l.firstname@email.com - d.john@email.com 
  * firstnamelastname12@email.com - johndoe12@email.com 


For a Senior Engineering Manager email, we recommend you either go with a custom domain name (john@johndoe.com) or select a very reputed email provider (Gmail or Outlook).
## How to Write a Professional Senior Engineering Manager Resume Summary?
Use this template to write the best Senior Engineering Manager resume summary: Senior Engineering Manager with [number of years] experience of [top 2-3 skills]. Achieved [top achievement]. Expert at [X], [Y] and [Z].
## How to Write a Senior Engineering Manager Resume Experience Section?
Here’s how you can write a job winning Senior Engineering Manager resume experience section:
  * Write your Senior Engineering Manager work experience in a reverse chronological order. 
  * Use bullets instead of paragraphs to explain your Senior Engineering Manager work experience. 
  * While describing your work experience focus on highlighting what you did and the impact you made (you can use numbers to describe your success as a Senior Engineering Manager). 
  * Use action verbs in your bullet points.

## How Long Should my Senior Engineering Manager Resume be?
Your Senior Engineering Manager resume length should be less than one or two pages maximum. Unless you have more than 25 years of experience, any resume that’s more than two pages would appear to be too long and risk getting rejected.
On an average, for Senior Engineering Manager, we see most resumes have a length of 2. And, that’s why we advise you to keep the resume length appropriate to not get rejected.
