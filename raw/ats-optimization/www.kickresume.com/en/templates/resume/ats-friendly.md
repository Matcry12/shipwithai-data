# Human-friendly or ATS-friendly resume templates?—Yes.
Our ATS-friendly resume templates are not only attractive and functional, but most importantly, they’ll get you hired. 
### What are ATS-friendly resume templates?
While it may sometimes look like an Application Terminating System, the abbreviation stands for an **Applicant Tracking System (ATS)**. It is a tool **employers and recruiters rely on to screen resumes**.
The way it works is after you submit your application, the **ATS analyzes your resume** in order to search for **relevant** **details**. The system then evaluates how closely your qualifications align with the job requirements, ranking your application accordingly. Based on that, only the top-ranked resumes move forward in the process.
To operate the ATS, recruiters enter details like the job description, required skills, and desired experience. Using this data, the software builds a profile of the ideal candidate. When reviewing applications, the ATS compares each one against this profile. At the end, **your ranking depends on how closely your resume matches that ideal**.
### Why ATS-friendly resumes matter
It's simple, because crafting a resume that resonates well with an ATS software is **crucial** for your success. Studies show that **over 90% of recruiters** in large-sized companies now rely on this technology during hiring campaigns, and this number keeps growing in mid and smaller-sized businesses as well.
Typically the first thing an applicant has to pass through in the hiring process is the initial ATS scan. But, if your resume doesn’t align with the system’s criteria, your application might be rejected before it even reaches a human person.
### How does ATS work?
First, the ATS software **scans key sections** of your resume, including your contact information, summary, work experience, and education. While doing so, it also searches for specific keywords that match the job requirements. Based on the software’s ranking system, **only the resumes that best fit the criteria move forward to the next stage**.
### What do ATS systems scan for?
An ATS software **scans various parts** of your resume to determine how well you meet the role's criteria. It’s important to understand the key parts of your application the ATS will specifically look at.
The main elements to keep in mind are: 
  * **The file type of your resume**
  * **[The specific keywords included](https://www.kickresume.com/en/blog/resume-keywords/)**
  * **[The headings and subheadings](https://www.kickresume.com/en/blog/resume-headings/)**


If you want to make sure your resume is optimized for ATS, you can test your resume using our [**ATS Resume Checker**](https://www.kickresume.com/en/ats-resume-checker/).

There are multiple ways how to achieve a well-optimized CV. We have a whole **[article](https://www.kickresume.com/en/blog/ats-friendly-resume/)** dedicated to this topic, but here’s a few tips (because we want you to succeed):
