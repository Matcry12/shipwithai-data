# ATS Keywords for Engineering Resume | Engineering Resume Buzzwords 
Featured In:
In today’s competitive job market, [optimizing your resume for Applicant Tracking Systems](https://www.visualcv.com/blog/how-to-beat-the-applicant-tracking-system/) (ATS) is crucial for engineering roles, where technical expertise, problem-solving skills, and project management capabilities are highly valued.
ATS software filters resumes based on specific keywords relevant to job descriptions. By incorporating keywords that reflect engineering competencies—such as CAD design, project management, and regulatory compliance—engineers can enhance their resume’s ATS visibility and attract the attention of hiring managers.
## Importance of Listing ATS Keywords in an Engineering Resume
Using relevant ATS keywords improves resume performance by:
  * Highlighting essential technical and analytical skills required for engineering roles.
  * Showcasing expertise in areas like design, testing, and quality assurance that align with hiring expectations.
  * Including proficiencies in industry-specific software and standards critical for engineering professionals.


## Top Keywords for Engineering Resumes
Engineers should include keywords that reflect core skills and responsibilities, such as:
  * Project Management
  * CAD Design (e.g., AutoCAD, SolidWorks)
  * Quality Assurance and Testing
  * Problem Solving
  * Risk Assessment
  * Compliance and Safety Standards
  * Product Development
  * Prototyping
  * Data Analysis
  * Lean Manufacturing


These keywords capture the essential skills and knowledge areas valuable across various engineering disciplines.
## ATS Keywords for Entry-Level Engineering Resume
For entry-level engineering roles, focus on foundational skills and general engineering support capabilities:
  * Technical Documentation
  * Testing and Troubleshooting
  * CAD and 3D Modeling
  * Basic Programming (e.g., MATLAB, Python)
  * Laboratory Experience
  * Research and Analysis
  * Report Writing
  * Team Collaboration
  * Data Collection
  * Quality Control


These keywords reflect competencies ideal for recent graduates or individuals beginning their engineering careers.
## ATS Keywords for Mechanical Engineering Resume
Mechanical engineers should incorporate keywords related to mechanical design, system analysis, and manufacturing:
  * Mechanical Design
  * Thermal Analysis
  * Finite Element Analysis (FEA)
  * Fluid Dynamics
  * Product Lifecycle Management (PLM)
  * HVAC Systems
  * CNC Machining
  * Stress Analysis
  * Mechanical Testing
  * Design for Manufacturing (DFM)


These keywords underscore the technical skills required for mechanical engineering roles.
## ATS Keywords for Electrical Engineering Resume
Electrical engineers should focus on keywords relevant to circuit design, electrical systems, and power distribution:
  * Circuit Design
  * PCB Layout
  * Power Systems
  * Signal Processing
  * Electromagnetic Compatibility (EMC)
  * AC/DC Systems
  * Microcontrollers and Embedded Systems
  * Control Systems
  * Electrical Testing and Troubleshooting
  * MATLAB and Simulink


These keywords demonstrate the technical expertise needed to work with electrical systems and components.
## ATS Keywords for Civil Engineering Resume
For civil engineering roles, emphasize keywords related to infrastructure, construction, and environmental compliance:
  * Structural Analysis
  * Geotechnical Engineering
  * Environmental Compliance
  * AutoCAD Civil 3D
  * Site Planning
  * Water Resources Management
  * Transportation Engineering
  * Soil Analysis
  * Project Estimation
  * Construction Management


These terms reflect the skills and knowledge essential for civil engineering positions focused on infrastructure and public works.
## ATS Keywords for Software Engineering Resume
Software engineers should include keywords that highlight coding skills, software development, and debugging:
  * Software Development
  * Coding and Programming Languages (e.g., Python, Java, C++)
  * Agile Methodologies
  * Software Testing and Debugging
  * Version Control (e.g., Git)
  * Full Stack Development
  * API Integration
  * Cloud Computing (e.g., AWS, Azure)
  * Database Management (e.g., SQL, MongoDB)
  * Object-Oriented Programming (OOP)


These keywords reflect the technical skills required for developing, testing, and deploying software.
## ATS Keywords for Chemical Engineering Resume
Chemical engineers should incorporate keywords relevant to chemical processes, safety, and regulatory compliance:
  * Process Design and Optimization
  * Chemical Safety and Compliance
  * Heat Transfer
  * Mass Balance Calculations
  * Reaction Engineering
  * Process Simulation (e.g., ASPEN Plus, HYSYS)
  * Quality Control
  * Waste Management
  * Environmental Regulations
  * Pilot Plant Operations


These keywords demonstrate specialized skills essential for chemical engineering roles.
## Tips for Listing ATS Keywords on an Engineering Resume
  * **Align with Job Descriptions:** Use keywords found in job descriptions to match ATS requirements.
  * **Integrate Keywords Naturally:** Place keywords seamlessly within job descriptions for a cohesive resume.
  * **Highlight Relevant Software and Tools:** List software such as **AutoCAD** , **SolidWorks** , **MATLAB** , **SAP** , **ANSYS** , **Revit** , or **Python** as part of your technical skills.
  * **Quantify Achievements:** Whenever possible, include metrics like “reduced production costs by 20%” or “improved efficiency by 30%.”
  * **Emphasize Relevant Certifications:** Mention certifications like **Professional Engineer (PE)** , **Certified Manufacturing Engineer (CMfgE)** , or **Lean Six Sigma Green Belt** for added credibility.


Following these tips will ensure your resume is optimized for ATS, making it well-suited for engineering roles across a variety of specializations.
