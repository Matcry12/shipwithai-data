## What is an applicant tracking system?

An applicant tracking system (ATS) automates recruitment by scanning resumes for businesses—think Fortune 500 companies or small firms alike. Instead of a hiring manager or employer sifting through stacks of resumes, ATS bots handle the job search grunt work. They sort dozens or thousands of applications quickly, saving time in the recruitment process.Related: How To Write an ATS-Friendly Resume## How does ATS work?

ATS scans resumes for essentials like contact information, job titles, and education history. It also hunts for specific keywords tied to the job listing—think “data entry” or “project management.” Resumes meeting minimum qualifications pass the ATS scanner and move to recruiters, while those lacking the right keywords get filtered out.Read more: What Kind of File Should Your Resume Be?## 6 tips for creating an ATS resume

Crafting an ATS-optimized resume requires avoiding pitfalls that confuse ATS software. Here are six practical tips for job seekers:### 1. Use an ATS resume template

ATS-friendly resume templates feature minimalist designs that work with ATS systems. These layouts ensure the resume scanner catches every detail—skills, certifications, and more—without missing critical keywords due to odd formatting. Look for pre-formatted options in tools like Google Docs or Microsoft Word.### 2. Label resume sections clearly

Use standard section headings like “Professional Summary,” “Work Experience,” and “Skills.” ATS relies on these labels to pinpoint your qualifications, such as years of experience or hard skills, so keep them straightforward.### 3. Avoid headers, tables, and graphics

ATS struggles with text boxes, columns, headers, or images. Skip these to ensure the system reads your full resume content. Stick to simple formatting for a clean .docx or PDF file type—check the job posting for accepted formats.### 4. Choose ATS-friendly fonts

Opt for standard fonts like Arial, Calibri, or Times New Roman in 10–12 point size. These are legible to both ATS software and humans, ensuring your resume stays scannable and professional.### 5. Apply keyword optimization

Study the job description for role-specific terms like “customer service” or “calendar management.” Naturally weave these resume keywords into your professional summary, skills section, and work experience. Include acronyms (e.g. “MBA”) alongside full terms (e.g. “Master of Business Administration”) for maximum reach—without stuffing.### 6. Pick the right file format

A .docx file is often safest for ATS compatibility, though some systems accept PDFs. Review the job application instructions to confirm. A resume builder can help you save in the correct file type.Related: How To Create an Applicant Tracking System Resume## ATS resume template

## FAQs

Here are brief answers to some frequently asked questions about this topic:### What makes a resume ATS-friendly?

An ATS-friendly resume uses simple formatting, clear section headings, and no graphics or tables. It weaves in relevant keywords naturally for the ATS scanner to catch.### Is there a resume format that’s most ATS-friendly?

Yes—the reverse chronological format works best. It lists work experience or education starting with the most recent, making it easy for ATS systems to parse.### How do I choose keywords for an ATS resume?

Scan the job listing for repeated or highlighted terms—like “job title” or “certifications.” Match these to your background and sprinkle them into your resume content.### Does resume length matter for ATS?

No—ATS scans focus on keywords and structure, not length.
