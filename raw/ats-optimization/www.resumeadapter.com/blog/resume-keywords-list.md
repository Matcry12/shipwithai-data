#  [](https://www.resumeadapter.com/blog/resume-keywords-list#why-do-resume-keywords-matter-in-2026)Why Do Resume Keywords Matter in 2026?
**The brutal truth:** Recruiters no longer manually read every resume.
They use **Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS)** that scan your document for:
  * ✅ **Skills** mentioned in the job description
  * ✅ **Tools and software** names (HubSpot, SAP, Figma, etc.)
  * ✅ **Industry-specific terms** (GAAP, Epic, Agile, etc.)
  * ✅ **Performance metrics** (ROI, KPIs, revenue growth)
  * ✅ **Role-specific terminology** (GTM strategy, reconciliation, triage)


**If your resume doesn't match the job's vocabulary, it gets filtered out often before a human ever sees it.**
According to [Society for Human Resource Management (SHRM)](https://www.shrm.org/) confirms that ATS keyword matching is the primary screening method for most organizations.

#  [](https://www.resumeadapter.com/blog/resume-keywords-list#faq)FAQ
##  [](https://www.resumeadapter.com/blog/resume-keywords-list#what-are-the-most-important-resume-keywords)What are the most important resume keywords?
The ones _inside the job description_ - ATS prioritizes exact matches.
##  [](https://www.resumeadapter.com/blog/resume-keywords-list#should-i-repeat-keywords)Should I repeat keywords?
Yes, but naturally. ATS rewards repetition across sections.
##  [](https://www.resumeadapter.com/blog/resume-keywords-list#are-software-tools-necessary)Are software tools necessary?
Absolutely. Tools like Figma, SAP, GA4, and Salesforce are high-value keywords.
##  [](https://www.resumeadapter.com/blog/resume-keywords-list#should-i-use-a-two-column-resume)Should I use a two-column resume?
No. ATS struggles with them.
##  [](https://www.resumeadapter.com/blog/resume-keywords-list#can-i-use-creative-templates)Can I use creative templates?
Avoid them unless applying directly via email to a human.
