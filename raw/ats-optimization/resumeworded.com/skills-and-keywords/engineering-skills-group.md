# How do I add skills to an Engineering resume?
1
Review the job posting closely.
Go through the Engineering posting you're applying to, and identify hard skills the company is looking for. For example, skills like JavaScript, Java and Git are possible skills. These are skills you should try to include on your resume.
[ ![](https://resumeworded.com/assets/images/resume-guides/how-to-images/tailor-resume-to-posting.jpg?Engineering) ](https://resumeworded.com/assets/images/resume-guides/how-to-images/tailor-resume-to-posting.jpg?Engineering)
2
Add industry skills like SQL and React.js.
Add other common skills from your industry - such as Software Development, C# and AngularJS - into your resume if they're relevant.
[ ![](https://resumeworded.com/skills-and-keywords/img/engineering.jpeg?Engineering) ](https://resumeworded.com/skills-and-keywords/img/engineering.jpeg?Engineering)
3
Add skills into your work experience.
Incorporate skills - like Cisco Routers, Petroleum and Jira - into your work experience too. This shows hiring managers that you have practical experience with these tools, techniques and skills.
[ ![](https://resumeworded.com/assets/images/resume-guides/how-to-images/weave-keywords-into-bullets.jpg?Engineering) ](https://resumeworded.com/assets/images/resume-guides/how-to-images/weave-keywords-into-bullets.jpg?Engineering)
4
Highlight technical and engineering skills.
It's important to show hiring managers your technical experience, whether that's in your previous Engineering roles or other experiences. Try to emphasize your technical and engineering experience through your bullet points.
[ ![](https://resumeworded.com/assets/images/resume-guides/how-to-images/engineering.jpg?Engineering) ](https://resumeworded.com/assets/images/resume-guides/how-to-images/engineering.jpg?Engineering)
5
Highlight any software engineering or development experience.
Programming and software development are common skill sets for Engineering roles, so try to highlight related accomplishments in your summary, work experience or skills sections.
[ ![](https://resumeworded.com/assets/images/resume-guides/how-to-images/software.jpeg?Engineering) ](https://resumeworded.com/assets/images/resume-guides/how-to-images/software.jpeg?Engineering)
6
Use the exact job title.
Try to add the exact job title, Engineering, somewhere into your resume to get past resume screeners. See the infographic for how to do this.
[ ![](https://resumeworded.com/assets/images/resume-guides/how-to-images/job-title-resume-title.jpg?Engineering) ](https://resumeworded.com/assets/images/resume-guides/how-to-images/job-title-resume-title.jpg?Engineering)

# Frequently Asked Questions
###  What are the top skills you should add to your Engineering resume? 
Some popular Engineering hard skills are Software Development, JavaScript, Java, Git, C#, AngularJS, SQL and React.js.   
  
Depending on the job you apply to, skills like Petroleum, VMware vSphere, Microsoft Access, Cisco Routers and Jira can also be good to include on your resume.
###  What are the differences in resume skills between a Logistics Engineer and a Drilling Engineer? 
If you are writing a Logistics Engineer resume, try to emphasize skills like Logistics Management, Logistics Engineering, Supply Chain Management, Supply Chain Optimization, Systems Engineering, Warehouse Operations, Continuous Improvement and Six Sigma.   
  
Similarly, if you are writing a Drilling Engineer resume, emphasize skills like Drilling Engineering, Drilling, Offshore Drilling, Drilling Fluids, Onshore Operations, Directional Drilling, Petroleum and Petroleum Engineering. 
###  What are good resume skills to include for different Engineering roles and job titles? 
Depending on the specific role you apply to, you may need to emphasize different skill sets. Here are a few examples:
  * **Drilling Engineer** : Drilling Engineering, Drilling, Offshore Drilling, Drilling Fluids and Onshore Operations
  * **Logistics Engineer** : Logistics Management, Logistics Engineering, Supply Chain Management, Supply Chain Optimization and Systems Engineering
  * **Site Reliability Engineer** : Ansible, Amazon Web Services (AWS), Docker Products, DevOps and Jenkins
