#  13 Software Developer Resume Examples - Here's What Works In 2026
##  Software developers are the magicians behind the applications we use daily on our mobiles or computers. They design, develop, and maintain programs by using programming languages. Most software developers are problem-solvers, logical thinkers, and lifelong learners, as this industry is always evolving.   
  
According to the Bureau of Labor Statistics, software developers are highly in-demand. Their job outlook is expected to increase by up to 25 percent, which is significantly high compared to other occupations. Yet, a good resume can elevate even more those expectations. This guide will help you create the best software developer resume.
