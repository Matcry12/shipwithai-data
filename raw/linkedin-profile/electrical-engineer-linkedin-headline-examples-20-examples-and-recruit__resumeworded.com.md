### Communicate Your Specialty

By stating you're skilled in PCB Design, RF Engineering, and AutoCAD, you're differentiating yourself from other electrical engineers. These specifics communicate your unique value and increase the likelihood of matching with recruiters looking for these particular skills.

### Leverage Past Experience

Having a former title like Power System Analyst at Siemens puts a spotlight on your experience and competence. It gives a sense of your professional journey and hints at your proficiency in a particular field.

### Express Your Passion

Stating you're "Passionate about developing energy-efficient systems" conveys your enthusiasm and dedication. It demonstrates your commitment to your field, which is appealing to potential employers.
