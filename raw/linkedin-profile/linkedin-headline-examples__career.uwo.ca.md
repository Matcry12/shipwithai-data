# LinkedIn Headline Examples

In a headline, consider including at least two of the following:

- Your current role
- Area of study
- Top skills or area(s) of expertise
- Notable accomplishments
- Career goal
- Industry of interest

## Here are some examples

PhD (c), Electrical & Computer Engineering, Interested in R&D related to the Internet of Things (IoT) networks

Honours English Literature Student | Writer, Editor, and Aspiring Publishing Professional

Chemical & Biochemical Engineer | Skilled in Wastewater Treatment | Interest in Research & Development

Western Law Student | Interested in Immigration and Personal Injury Law | Dean’s List 2019 | Faculty of Law Blog Editor
