# Amazon refusing to negotiate intern return offer
I received a return offer after my internship with AWS and that was before Amazon had improved their packages or at least so I believe according to the levels data. My offer was never refreshed or improved upon after that and if you check out the data, the new grad offers there now are well above 200k like even in the 230s and 220s. I have competing offers which are higher and I also was an intern who added value to the company. Why can’t I get a similar offer to someone who just applied off the RIP and has the same 0 years of experience as me? Doesn’t make much sense. Tried negotiating and I’m being told Student Programs offers are standard and non-negotiable.
