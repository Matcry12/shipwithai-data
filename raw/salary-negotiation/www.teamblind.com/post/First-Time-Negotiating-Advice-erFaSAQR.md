# First Time Negotiating Advice 
This is my first time switching jobs / getting an offer after the first job I started post college. Went through the interview process and got an offer but I want to negotiate the base and title. But not sure how to go about it? The recruiter and hiring manager emailed me indicating if I have any questions to reach out to them - who should I be talking to about this? Also how do I even bring up the subject? I don’t want to come off as greedy or lose the opportunity. Any advice appreciated. Thank you. You must absolutely negotiate. Remember that most recruiters and companies expect you to negotiate and have probably rolled out a slightly low-balled offer to you based on that expectation. It will never be perceived as greedy (unless you quote a ridiculous number in the counter negotiation) and very rarely have I heard of offers getting rescinded because of negotiation (applies to most mid to big size companies, can’t say much about smaller companies as don’t have much data on that). So go for it! As for how to go about it, firstly, research well on what is the reasonable number you should be asking for. [Levels.fyi](http://Levels.fyi) can be a useful resource for that. Of course take into account factors such as your YoE, location, competing offers you can leverage if any, etc. Then tell them a number higher than what you would be happy with. You can reply to the individual who first put forward the offer to you..which in most cases is the recruiter. You can start the email by saying “Thank you so much for your offer, I am very excited about joining the team. For the compensation, based on some of the market data I have seen for my yoe and skill set, I was looking for a number closer to XXX. I am really hoping that we can work together to get the total compensation up to that number…” Mention that you are excited about joining and want to come in for the long term feeling happy and fairly compensated. They will likely reply back with a number that is between what you asked and what was first offered. You can maybe ask them again if the number is still too low or accept if the new number works for you. There are some really good resources online for help with negotiation in tech. This is one I found really helpful - <https://candor.co/guides/salary-negotiation> Hope this helps! Good luck!

Don’t give a number or a range at all. Let them give you the first number. Then respectfully say that you’re expectation was higher and it doesn’t make sense to make a move with this package. They will ask you what range you want. Give them a number (TC) that you know you’re not gonna get but it is reasonable. They will come back to you with something higher. If you’re happy take it and if not say still there is a big gap and you want this much equity, bonus etc. always finish negotiating base first and then move to equity and bonus. Always have a number in your mind that below that you would walk out and another number that above that you would accept. This helps you not to get greedy and also not get disappointed with the negotiations. I’m not an expert and wanna hear other folks inputs.
