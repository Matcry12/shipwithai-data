How to negotiate salary on return offer post internship? Hello, I’m currently finishing up my summer internship and I have been offered a full time role upon graduation (may 2025) how should I go about negotiating salary? Or is it even worthwhile? The offer is 70k, but I’m looking for 80k. The position would be in network engineering/cybersecurity. Chicago based. Thanks for your help.

Hi there. Looking at the current market, it’s very competitive and really hard to find any entry level positions. It might be a good opportunity you don’t wanna regret later. Check how much other companies offer for similar entry level positions around you. If that salary makes sense compared to current average salary offered by other companies it might worth considering. If you have a good relationship with the manager (which I think so as you already got an offer) you may just tell your expectations. I don’t think that will be a reason for a rejection all together. I jumped on my first offer in cybersecurity with 60K. At the end of my first year, they increased my salary to $75K. If I were you I’d ask, if they don’t accept I’d accept the offer and keep applying other higher paying companies. If no offer is gotten by your graduation, at least there is a company willing to employ you

Have multiple offers and confidence.

Typically you don’t. Most firms don’t negotiate salary on a return offer.
