Tips for negotiating a software engineering salary

Salary negotiations can seem daunting, especially if you’re unsure whether the package aligns with your worth (or industry standards). When negotiating your software engineering salary, it’s important to remember that, generally, what you put in is what you get out. If you take the time to do your research and build a unique value proposition, you can be on your way to securing fair compensation for your skills and services. Ready to beat the negotiation anxiety? Be sure to try our four tips below:

Know your worth

Understanding the prevailing salary trends will help you to navigate negotiations so you can be confident in your worth and make a compelling case.Undertake your own research and consider how the offer fits in the context of the wider market. The 2025 Robert Half Salary Guide offers comprehensive salary data and insights to help you determine whether an offer is within the market range for your experience and position level. If the offer is below the market averages or you feel you are worth more, it’s important to justify this to your employer or prospective employer. Sam encourages candidates to be realistic. He says, “I want software engineers to secure a top salary. However, I’d also encourage them to negotiate from an informed position. Ask yourself whether you’re in a position to negotiate a higher salary or whether you need to build on some skills or qualifications to hit a more advanced salary range.”“Also, acknowledge regional differences (as outlined in The Robert Half Salary Guide). In doing so, you’ll know where you have room to negotiate based on your location. For example, a software engineer in Sydney can expect a salary 8% above the national average, whereas a software engineer in Melbourne or Brisbane can only expect a salary that’s 2% above the national average,” he says.

Prepare for negotiation

Before you commence your negotiations, take the time to distil your value by evaluating your current role, job history, skills and contributions. Be prepared to justify your desired salary - use quantifiable evidence to highlight your achievements, professional growth activities, and future aspirations while aligning them to the organisation’s objectives. Sam says, “Gather any evidence that will help to sharpen your competitive edge – think recent achievements, industry certifications or any niche skill sets that may increase your business appeal.” “While hard skills are critical, it’s important to call out your soft skills too. Some of the most successful software engineers are those who are efficient, effective and empathetic. This approach makes them stellar communicators, project managers and collaborators.”

Master the conversation

Sam believes preparation, confidence and strategic communication can help you master the conversation during your software engineer salary negotiation. He says, “I always encourage professionals to leverage scripts or templates tailored for common negotiation scenarios. They can help you to articulate your value, respond to challenging questions and address counteroffers.”“I work with software engineers who have prepared scripts outlining their skills, experience and market trends to justify their desired salary. It’s an effective way to stay composed and persuasive. At the very least, practising responses to tough questions can help to field interview questions with ease.”Remember, negotiation success hinges on how you conduct yourself, so be sure to maintain a collaborative yet confident approach. Remain polite, positive, and professional throughout the process - be open to compromises and feedback, but take charge of the situation positively to help assert your confidence and preparedness for further negotiations.

Leverage market trends

2025 is set to bring about some significant market trends. As outlined in the latest Robert Half Salary Guide:Job security remains a top priority for IT and tech employees following market volatility. 41% of tech employees prioritise job security over salary in the current market.Experts in infrastructure, service and security are expected to be most sought after as companies seek to optimise their security systems and capitalise on new technology to stay competitive.Financial services, healthcare and aged care, manufacturing, transport and mining services are among the sectors most heavily recruiting for tech professionals in Australia.As companies migrate to cloud-based software solutions, there is high demand for skilled cloud integration engineers who can transition businesses from on-premises to cloud environments.Rather than committing to permanent hires, employers are opting for contractors to address urgent demands. 73% of Australian businesses plan to hire tech contractors in the next 12 months for new or existing positions, predominately in the areas of security, application development and networking. With all of this in mind, Sam says it’s important to leverage market trends in your software engineer salary negotiation. He says, “These patterns should guide the direction of your negotiations. For example, the shift towards remote work has significantly impacted pay expectations, creating opportunities to negotiate based on flexibility and cost-of-living adjustments.”“Additionally, staying informed about in-demand skills like cloud computing and cybersecurity can strengthen your position, as employers often pay a premium for expertise in these areas.” Above all, Sam says it’s important to consider the value beyond salary. “During any salary negotiation, it’s important to evaluate the package as a whole. Salary isn’t the only factor to consider - it’s important to assess what additional benefits could form the best package for you. Things like flexible work hours, hybrid working models and extra annual leave entitlements can help to enrich your employee experience,” he says. As we enter 2025, leaders want to know they have the best IT talent protecting and building their business. With the right information and the right preparation, you can navigate these critical salary conversations with confidence. Say goodbye to negotiation anxiety and hello to powerful persuasion.
