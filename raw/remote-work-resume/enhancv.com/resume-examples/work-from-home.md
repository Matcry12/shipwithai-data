Crafting a resume for work-at-home jobs might seem like you're merely swapping office shoes for slippers, but as the workspace extends into the digital realm, the stakes are surprisingly high. With a staggering [98% of workers expressing a desire to work remotely at least some of the time](https://www.forbes.com/advisor/business/remote-work-statistics/), tailoring your resume for remote roles is more crucial than ever. 
This overwhelming preference for flexibility and autonomy in the workplace requires a resume that highlights your skills and demonstrates your adeptness at thriving in a self-directed environment.
Whether you’re targeting a creative field or a more traditional sector, your resume must convey that you’re fully equipped for the future of work—a future where remote jobs aren’t just a niche but a norm. Let’s explore [how to construct a resume](https://enhancv.com/blog/how-to-write-a-resume/) that glows in a world where almost everyone wants to log in from home.
![Checklist icon](https://enhancv.com/resume-examples/work-from-home/)
##### Key takeaways
  * The reverse chronological format is recommended for remote roles to highlight career progression and remote management capabilities.
  * Resume design should ensure readability with professional colors and clear layouts, ideal for showcasing remote work tools and technologies.
  * Experience sections should be adapted to reflect remote work capabilities, using concise bullet points and quantifiable metrics.
  * When tailoring your resume to remote jobs, highlight your ability to thrive in a self-directed environment while ensuring it aligns with the job description and proper formatting for ATS and recruiter approval.
  * Incorporating a functional or hybrid resume format can benefit those with limited remote work experience by emphasizing relevant skills.
  * Certifications related to remote work, such as Certified Remote Work Professional, enhance a resume by validating expertise in necessary remote tools and practices.

## How to Format a Work-from-home Resume
Creating a well-structured and professional [resume layout](https://enhancv.com/blog/resume-layout/) is crucial for making [an all-around good resume](https://enhancv.com/blog/what-makes-a-good-resume/) that grabs the attention of recruiters. This section will guide you through the best practices for [resume formats](https://enhancv.com/blog/resume-format/) tailored to work-from-home job applicants, ensuring it meets industry standards and effectively demonstrates your skills and experience.
### Best resume format
  * [Reverse chronological format](https://enhancv.com/blog/reverse-chronological-resume/)**:** This format is highly recommended for work-from-home roles, emphasizing your work experience and career progression which is essential for showing your ability to manage responsibilities remotely.


### Resume design
  * Keep [resume margins](https://enhancv.com/blog/resume-margins/) between 0.5 to 1 inch to ensure readability.
  * Use business [colors on your resume](https://enhancv.com/blog/color-on-resume/), primarily black and white, with minimal use of accent colors for [resume headings](https://enhancv.com/blog/resume-headings/) to maintain a clean appearance.
  * [Choose clean, professional fonts](https://enhancv.com/blog/resume-font/) like Arial, Calibri, or Times New Roman, sized 10 to 12 points.
  * Consider [a double-column resume layout](https://enhancv.com/resume-templates/two-column/) to maintain clarity and ease of reading, especially important when listing remote work tools and technologies.
  * Aim for [a one-page resume](https://enhancv.com/blog/one-page-resume/), but [two pages are acceptable](https://enhancv.com/blog/two-page-resume/) if you have extensive experience relevant to remote work.


### Resume header
  * In your [resume header](https://enhancv.com/blog/perfecting-resume-header/), be sure to give your [contact information](https://enhancv.com/blog/contact-information-on-resume/), including your city and state, but [omit the full address](https://enhancv.com/blog/address-on-resume/) as a full-time home location is less relevant for remote jobs.
  * Provide a professional email address, [LinkedIn profile](https://enhancv.com/blog/how-to-put-linkedin-on-your-resume/), and personal website or portfolio if relevant, which can be linked to remote work projects or tools you’re proficient with.
  * Generally, [avoid including a photo](https://enhancv.com/blog/resume-photo/) unless explicitly requested or relevant to the job market in your country.
  * Create a concise [resume headline](https://enhancv.com/blog/resume-headline/) that summarizes your professional identity and highlights your adaptability and proficiency in remote environments.


### File formatting
  * [Save your resume as a PDF](https://enhancv.com/blog/resume-in-pdf-or-word/) to preserve formatting.
  * Use “FirstName _LastName_ Remote_Resume.pdf” as a [naming convention](https://enhancv.com/blog/what-to-name-your-resume-file/) for clarity and to underscore your focus on remote work.

## How to Best Write Your Work-from-home Resume Experience
When crafting [the work experience section](https://enhancv.com/blog/resume-work-experience/) for a work-from-home resume, it's important to adapt your entries to reflect remote work capabilities and the nuances of virtual environments. 
Here’s how to structure each entry to illustrate your suitability for a remote role:
  * Set the stage for your experience by clarifying your role, the company, and the fact that you worked remotely.
  * [Use concise bullet points](https://enhancv.com/blog/resume-bullet-points-or-paragraphs/) to describe key responsibilities and achievements, particularly those that demonstrate your ability to work independently and effectively in a remote setting. Don’t forget to begin each point with [an action verb](https://enhancv.com/blog/resume-action-verbs/).
  * Specify any software, collaboration tools, or methodologies used, particularly those relevant to remote work, such as project management platforms, communication tools, and virtual collaboration techniques.
  * Whenever possible, quantify results to demonstrate the direct impact of your contributions, especially those that showcase your ability to succeed in a remote environment.
  * [Your work experience shouldn’t extend further back than 10-15 years](https://enhancv.com/blog/how-far-back-should-your-resume-go/), focusing on roles that display your skills and achievements in remote work contexts. This ensures your resume remains concise, impactful, and relevant to the needs of employers seeking remote workers.


Tailoring your resume for remote roles is essential because it increases relevance by matching your skills and experiences with the specific requirements of the job, proving you are an ideal fit for working from home.

## How Do I Write a Work-from-home Resume with No Experience?
Writing a work-from-home [resume without direct experience](https://enhancv.com/blog/resume-without-work-experience/) can be a challenge, but with the right format and focus on your relevant skills and potential, you can create a compelling application. 
Here are some key strategies and structures to consider:
  * [Functional resume](https://enhancv.com/blog/functional-skill-based-resume/)**:** This format emphasizes your skills and abilities rather than your chronological work history. It’s ideal for those new to the workforce, career changers, or individuals with gaps in their employment.
  * [Hybrid resume](https://enhancv.com/blog/hybrid-resume/)**:** Combines elements of both chronological and functional formats, showcasing relevant skills at the top of the resume followed by a brief work history. This format is useful for candidates who have some relevant skills but limited professional experience.


Besides choosing a different more relevant resume format, you can also add extra sections to compensate for your lack of experience.
Such as:
  1. Focus on [transferable skills](https://enhancv.com/resume-skills/transferable/) such as communication, time management, and proficiency in tech tools like Microsoft Office and CRM software.
  2. Mention any [school or personal projects](https://enhancv.com/blog/projects-on-resume/) relevant to the job, particularly those that demonstrate your ability to manage tasks or coordinate with others remotely.
  3. Select [a traditional template](https://enhancv.com/resume-templates/traditional/) to maintain a professional appearance, even without work experience.
  4. Enroll in relevant online courses or certification programs that focus on remote work tools and practices to build foundational knowledge.
  5. Gain practical experience by participating in virtual [internships](https://enhancv.com/blog/how-to-list-an-internship-on-your-resume/) or freelance projects that allow you to work remotely.
  6. Join [professional organizations](https://enhancv.com/blog/affiliations-on-resume/) related to telecommuting and remote work, and participate in online forums and webinars to show commitment and gain industry knowledge.
  7. Emphasize your ability to work independently, manage remote projects, and maintain productivity in a home office setting.


### Objective statement tips
[An objective statement](https://enhancv.com/blog/resume-objective/) is a brief and concise declaration placed at the top of a resume. It outlines the applicant's career goals and how they align with the company's needs, aiming to capture the employer's interest. Ideal for an applicant without any professional experience.
  * **Be specific:** Tailor your objective statement to the job and company, indicating your enthusiasm for the role and how you plan to contribute.
  * **Focus on company benefit:** Express how you can add value to the company, referencing specific skills or traits that match with the job description.

## How to List Your Hard and Soft Skills on Your Resume
Demonstrating your skills on a work-at-home resume emphasizes your ability to effectively manage tasks, communicate virtually, and maintain productivity independently. These skills aim to illustrate both your technical proficiency and your adaptability, which are crucial for successfully completing projects and collaborating remotely.
### Hard skills vs. soft skills
  * [Hard skills](https://enhancv.com/blog/hard-skills-on-resume/)**:** These are specific, teachable abilities or skill sets that are easily quantifiable and often verified through certifications and practical experience.
  * [Soft skills](https://enhancv.com/blog/soft-skills-on-resume/)**:** These are interpersonal attributes and behaviors that enhance your ability to work independently and as part of a team in a virtual environment. Shown through your past remote work experiences and are transferable across various remote job roles.

## How to Write Your Work-from-home Resume Summary
