# How To Demonstrate You're a Team Player on Your Resume

Written by

Updated February 28, 2026

Many industries rely on effective collaboration to maintain a productive output. Because of this, many employers and hiring managers look for evidence of teamwork skills on a candidate's resume. Understanding how to demonstrate you're a team player on your resume can help you impress the hiring manager and increase your chances of getting invited to interview. In this article, we discuss team player resumes, identify when to use one, highlight how to demonstrate you're a team player on a resume and what to include, and provide tips and an example as a guide.

## What is a team player resume?

A team player resume outlines and emphasizes the ability of a candidate to work in a team. It contains work experience and skills that demonstrate that a candidate can collaborate effectively in a work environment with others. As many industries prioritize teamwork, you're likely to need a teamwork resume during your career. Including teamwork skills on your resume can increase your chances of success in your application.Related jobs on Indeed

## When to use a teamwork resume

Most employers value effective employee collaboration, so including teamwork skills is always a good idea. You can customize your resume into a teamwork resume if the hiring manager requests. Study the job description thoroughly, and look out for words like "teamwork" and "team player" to determine if you need this type of resume for the role.You can submit a teamwork resume for roles that require collaboration, regardless of whether the hiring manager mentions it. For example, certain roles, like engineering, marketing, and software development, usually require working with colleagues and other professionals. In such cases, the hiring manager may expect you to assume teamwork skills are an essential part of the role. You can also research the company you're applying to and study their work model. Some companies take a more collaborative approach than others and applying to such companies with a "works well with others" resume can improve your chances of success.## How to demonstrate you're a team player on your resume

Here is how to explain that you're a team player on your resume:### 1. List all relevant examples of teamwork

It may be a challenge to remember all relevant examples of your teamwork skills. Do your best to identify examples that involve showing this soft skill. Listing tangible examples of teamwork for your include all important details. List examples of when you demonstrated teamwork, the project you were working on, and your teamwork skills' impact on the project's success.### 2. Include it on your resume introduction

Your resume introduction is one of the first sections designed to attract the hiring manager's attention. If you're creating a teamwork resume, include your teamwork skills at the beginning. You can mention your teamwork skills with other work duties or projects using words like "collaborated" or "co-operated." Similarly, you can demonstrate teamwork skills by including team projects you led or supervised. You can also mention teamwork skills as part of a list of skills in the resume introduction.### 3. Outline it below your work experience

Your work experience section is a great opportunity to cite team projects or tasks where you demonstrated teamwork skills. You can include your teamwork skills in the job descriptions in your work experience section. You can use action words like "collaborated" or "co-ordinated" to merge teamwork skills with other tasks or duties. When making this resume, ensure you prioritize experiences that required teamwork skills. State the task or challenge you faced, the role you played, how you demonstrated teamwork skills, and the results of your input. Ensure you also include details about your team.### 4. Mention it under the skills section

You can also mention your skills under the skill section to create a teamwork skills resume. Include teamwork skills on a bulleted list, along with your other skills. Be sure you mention the word "team" when including your skills because of keyword scans. You can also include individual teamwork skills like communication and conflict management.## What to include in resume to show teamwork

Here are some details you can include in this type of resume:### Leadership roles

The ability to lead a team effectively is evidence of good teamwork skills. Leaders need to learn the strengths and weaknesses of their teammates to lead them effectively. To demonstrate your skills, you can include examples of leading a team, project, unit, or department. Ensure you demonstrate how you used these skills during your leadership experience and their impact on your results. When citing leadership roles, ensure you specify the composition of the team. This includes the number of persons, their roles, and the extent of your duties as a leader.### Team projects

If you have little experience in leadership roles, you can include examples of yourself functioning as a team member. Ensure you describe details of your role in the team and how you used teamwork skills to achieve results or navigate any challenges. Include specific examples and quantify the impact of your contribution to the team. You can also describe the team's composition to give the hiring manager a good idea of the team dynamics and help them better appreciate your collaboration skills.### Awards or honours

Awards or honours that testify to your skills are a great addition to your resume. This can be leadership awards or awards directly on leadership. Performance-based awards that consider teamwork one of the performance metrics are also great additions to a teamwork resume. Ensure you include the name of the award, the issuer, and the date you received it. While all awards are special, prioritizing prestigious awards from reputable issuers or employers can improve your resume.### Team player keywords

To transform your resume into a teamwork-oriented one, you need to incorporate keywords about collaboration. These keywords help highlight your partnership skills and they also make it easier for your resume to pass through keyword scans. First, read through the job description to determine which keywords the hiring manager used, as those are the ones you need to include on your resume. Then, incorporate these words and phrases into every section of your resume for the best results.## Tips for creating an excellent resume

Here are some tips for drafting an excellent resume that demonstrates your collaboration skills:### Use specific examples

Rather than just mentioning that you have teamwork skills, use specific examples where you demonstrated them. This allows you to explain the impact of your skills and convince the hiring manager you're honest. When using specific examples, you can adopt the STAR or PAR approach. STAR means situation, task, approach, and result, while PAR means problem, approach, and result. Both are methods for conveniently outlining your skills and the results they had.### Be detailed

Provide all relevant details about your experiences that demonstrate teamwork. Relevant details include the team composition, your role in the team, when you used teamwork skills, and the overall result. Including these details helps provide a context for the hiring manager and allows them to better appreciate your skills.### Quantify your achievements

Quantifying your achievements is a great way to demonstrate the impact of your teamwork skills. This is particularly important for this type of resume, as that's the only way to measure how effective you are during collaboration. Use numerical measures like figures, percentages, ratios, and averages to quantify your achievements. You can also opt for adjectives like "excellent" or "high-quality."### Credit your teammates

While mentioning your teamwork skills already implies that you worked with others, mentioning your teammates can be a subtle way to demonstrate teamwork. Hiring managers might take notice of those as proof of genuineness. When citing examples of your team projects, acknowledge the contributions of others to the results. Remember that it's your resume, so the focus still needs to be on you.## Team player sample resume
