# Remote Work Resume: Templates and Samples
Written by
Indeed Editorial Team
Updated December 12, 2025
With modern technology and new online careers, many professionals are interested in remote work. Whether you have remote experience or are interested in applying for your first remote job, it's important to create a resume that reflects your qualifications. A great remote-job resume reflects your experience and skills and could help you stand out to recruiters or hiring managers. In this article, we provide three resume templates and samples you can use to help you create your resume for a new remote position.

## Resume templates for remote work experience
The three most popular [resume formats](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/resumes-cover-letters/resume-format-guide-with-examples) are chronological, functional and combination. You can choose whichever template works best for your career level and skills. Here are templates of all three formats to help you create your own remote work resume:
### Chronological resume template
A chronological resume, also known as a reverse chronological resume, lists your relevant work experience starting with your most recent role. ### Functional resume template
The functional resume format focuses more on your [relevant skills](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/resumes-cover-letters/best-resume-skills) than your job history. If you have a gap in your work experience or are switching careers, this could be a good format to prioritize your transferable achievements.
