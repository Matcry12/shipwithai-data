People may consider changing their careers to enhance their skills or work in a different environment. Becoming a software engineer may be a good choice for someone who enjoys technology, collaboration and troubleshooting. If you want to pursue a career in technology, learning how to make a career change to software engineering may be useful for you. In this article, we explain why you might want to change your career to software engineering, provide the steps you can take to do so and offer some useful tips.
## How to make a career change to a software engineer role
Here are the steps you can take that describe how to make a career change to a software engineer job:
### 1. Search for mentorship programs
A mentor may help you learn the skills and expectations for software engineering. Consider connecting with someone who is familiar with the industry. You can also ask your mentor about their common duties and skills in the software engineering field. This may help you determine if this field is a good match for you. Here are some ways to find professionals who can mentor you in software engineering:
  * Join an open-source project: Consider joining an online community that works on open-source projects. This can help you hone your code-writing and experience the collaborative environment you might experience in professional software development.
  * Ask friends and professional references: You can ask the students and professors you meet in programming courses if they know of any tutoring or mentoring programs. You can also collaborate on a personal project with more experienced friends.
  * Join online mentorship programs: Try researching online software mentorship programs, both paid and free. You can find testimonials from other people who received a mentorship to see if the program is relevant to your needs and goals.

Related: [What Is Software Development: Definition, Processes and Types](https://au.indeed.com/career-advice/career-development/software-development)
### 2. Decide on the position you want to pursue
After working with a mentor, try to decide on the software engineering position that you want to pursue. This can help you determine the qualifications, including education and certifications, that you may earn to secure the position. As an entry-level or mid-level technology professional, you might consider pursuing positions which include a traditional web developer, software engineer, software tester, cloud administrator and database analyst. Consider contacting professionals that work in these positions to better understand the benefits and potential challenges in their positions.Related: [How to Become a Software Developer](https://au.indeed.com/career-advice/finding-a-job/how-to-become-a-software-developer)
### 3. Complete the required education
Once you decide on the software engineering position that you want to pursue, look to complete the required education. Typically, most technology professionals complete a bachelor's degree in software engineering or computer science. If you have a degree in a different field, some of your credits may transfer to your new degree. For example, if you completed a teaching degree, some of your maths and science courses may count towards your engineering degree.If you have a degree in a related technology field, like a bachelor's degree in information technology, consider pursuing a master's degree in software engineering. Be sure to meet with university administration before pursuing a degree program to understand which of your existing credits count towards the program. If you want to continue working in your current position while completing your degree, look for educational institutions that offer part-time programs.
### 4. Improve your technical skills
To prepare for software engineering responsibilities, you can improve your technical skills. You can do so by reviewing coding materials, studying computer systems and researching industry standards. Here are some other ways you can strengthen your technical skills:
  * Review job descriptions. To prepare for technical interviews when applying for software engineering positions, research job descriptions for software engineering roles, review common technical interview questions and practise discussing software processes.
  * Enrol in courses that can enhance your skills. While completing your degree, try to find courses that may challenge you and grow your technical skills. You can research each program's curriculum to find classes that help you learn the basics of software engineering, strengthen specific skills or prepare you for in-person technical interviews.
  * Look for areas for improvement. As you transition into a software engineering career, look for areas for improvement in your skill set. Ask for feedback from your peers, management, professors and colleagues on specific skills that you can enhance to improve your performance.


### 5. Create a solid resume
When entering the software engineering field, it's important to have a solid resume that shows you're an excellent candidate. Since you may have limited experience in the technology field, use a resume format that showcases your skills, education and goals. For example, you might share that your goals are to develop high-performing technology or troubleshoot complex issues.Be sure to share your strong technical skills by including a list of technology-related abilities, like data analysis or computer programming. When you describe your work experience in your previous positions, try to emphasise the specific technical decisions you made, the clients you serviced, the types of technology you used and the types of projects you completed. Even if you worked in a different role or industry before, discussing your relevant technical abilities shows that you have valuable skills you can use as a software engineer.Related:


### 6. Craft a portfolio
Typically, hiring managers review a candidate's portfolio to gain an understanding of their software engineering experience and technology skills. Consider creating a portfolio that has samples of your software engineering projects and related projects from your previous jobs. You can attach a link to an online portfolio in your application while applying for positions.If you have limited experience in the technology field, you can include projects you worked on while completing your software engineering degree. You may also include projects from your recent jobs that demonstrate your transferable skills. For example, if you previously worked as a project manager, you might include a sample of a project that you managed to show your leadership skills, close attention to detail and organisational abilities.

## Why might you make a career change to software engineering?
You might make a career change to software engineering for several reasons, including the team-oriented work environment and technology-based projects in this new profession. You may be considering a career change that allows you to do the following:
### Collaborate on a team
While many technology careers involve independent work, software engineering frequently involves collaboration. Software engineers can work with a team of technology professionals, including software testers and developers, to create new software and maintain existing systems. You may use and develop collaborative skills to grow positive relationships with your team members while completing projects.Related: [What Does a Software Engineer Do? (Plus How to Become One)](https://au.indeed.com/career-advice/finding-a-job/what-does-a-software-engineer-do)
### Use problem-solving skills
If you enjoy solving complex issues, being a software engineer may be a good career choice for you. These professionals often perform troubleshooting to identify system issues and diagnose technical problems. A large part of their role includes analysing programs and applications to identify potential problems or risks. This may be a rewarding part of your career since you can assist clients and team members in resolving their software issues.
### Work in a variety of tasks
The software engineering field includes various software principles, topics and tasks that professionals can complete. You may work in a variety of roles as a software engineer, depending on your expertise and preference. Working with various topics may engage you throughout the workday and help you gain experience across multiple work areas that ultimately allow you to select special areas of career interest. For example, you might choose to specialise in working with computer applications, mobile software or networking, depending on your technology preference.Related: [How Much Does a Software Engineer Make? (With Common Duties)](https://au.indeed.com/career-advice/pay-salary/how-much-does-software-engineer-make)

Here are some tips to help you change your career to a software engineer:
### Grow your network

For example, a member of your network may share about a recent job opening for a software engineering position at their workplace. To expand your network, consider joining a software engineering society, like the [Australian Computer Society](https://www.acs.org.au/). You might also grow your network by attending software workshops and conferences. ### Ask for advice
Consider asking a member of your network to look over your resume to find areas of improvement. It may be useful to ask a professional in the technology field to review your resume, as they may be familiar with crafting resumes for software-related positions. Ask for feedback to ensure that you display your technical and transferable skills effectively throughout your resume.
