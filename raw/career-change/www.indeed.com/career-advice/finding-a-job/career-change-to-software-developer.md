## What does a software developer do?
A software developer designs and builds software programs for computers that allow users to perform specified tasks. Software developers can specialize in software that works on a single computer or programs that run on computer systems and control networks. Common duties for software developers include:
  * Developing and testing computer software
  * Leading a team of programmers to complete software programmers
  * Creating models and diagrams to help demonstrate the needed code to programmers
  * Monitoring a software's performance and quality
  * Recording code and tests for future reference

## How to make a career change to become a software developer
Here are some steps for changing to a software developer career:
### 1. Determine your existing obligations
When changing careers, you may already have existing time or financial obligations, such as a house, family or current job. These factors can influence your education options and job interview strategies. For example, if you currently have a job and need to pursue further education for a software developer role, taking a short intensive course you can complete during your time off may work better for you than taking traditional college courses.
### 2. Learn to code
Coding is a key part of a software developer's duties. You can teach yourself to code or learn through a variety of course options, such as online classes or coding boot camps. If you don't already have a bachelor's degree, you can also consider pursuing a degree in computer science and taking coding classes at your college.Read more: [20 Ways You Can Learn To Code](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/career-development/learn-to-code)
### 3. Take software development courses
Once you know some basic coding languages, try to take classes that are specific to software development. These may include data structure design, model or diagram design and development fundamentals. You can find courses online, at coding boot camps or software development immersive programs or at colleges as part of your computer science degree coursework.
### 4. Find a mentor
A mentor is a personal contact in the software development industry that may help you start your new career. Mentors can give advice, help you practice for interviews and tell you about job or networking opportunities. If you already know a software developer, consider asking them to become your mentor. Otherwise, you can find mentors by contacting software development companies, taking programming classes or boot camps or attending software development events, such as "hackathons."
### 4. Freelance or volunteer
Completing software development projects might help you improve your skills, gain experience and get hired as a developer. Consider volunteering to create software programs for local organizations or advertising your services as a freelancer. You may also use the programs you create for freelance or volunteer work in your portfolio.
### 5. Build a portfolio
A portfolio is a collection of your work that you can send to employers alongside your resume. Software developers usually have a digital portfolio to display their programs and other pieces of code. Try to create a portfolio for yourself and add your strongest freelance and volunteer material to demonstrate your software development experience.
### 6. Rewrite your resume
Before you apply for software developer jobs, rewrite your resume to better fit your new career field. Think about skills or character traits valuable to software developers that your current or former jobs helped you establish and try to highlight those on your resume. You can also include your new programming credentials and a link to your portfolio.
### 7. Study for system design interviews
Many software developer job interviews follow the system design interview process. System design interviews test your ability to create software programs and test for errors by giving you sample coding tasks to complete. Preparing for system design interviews in advance might increase your comfort in the format, help you gain experience solving coding challenges and improve your chances of getting hired.
### 8. Apply for jobs
After you prepare your portfolio and resume, you can start to apply for software developer jobs. You can usually find opportunities on online job sites or through professional networks. When searching on job sites, consider using search terms such as:
  * Software developer
  * Developer
  * Software development engineer


## Tips for becoming a software developer
Here are some suggestions to help you successfully change your career to become a software developer:
### Participate in a training program or boot camp
Software developers need to understand programming and software development principles. While you may consider earning a computer science degree, many people who change careers attend software development immersive training programs or coding boot camps. These short programs offer intensive classes that can teach you key software development skills. Professionals often choose one of these options because you can typically complete them in a short period of time and plan them around your work schedule.
### Get a bachelor's degree
Many software development positions require you to have a bachelor's degree. If you have a strong portfolio and resume, your degree can be in any major. For instance, if you have a four-year degree from your previous career, you can use your education as part of your qualifications. Otherwise, consider pursuing a degree in computer science or a related field.
### Expand your network
Networks can help you find job interview opportunities, collaborate with other software developers and practice for system design interviews. You may already have a network from your previous or current career that can connect you to people in the software development industry. Other ways to expand your network include talking to your college professors or coding boot camp teachers, joining coding or software development interest groups online and attending software development events such as hackathons or conventions.
### Attend hackathons
Hackathons are short programming events where groups of programmers, graphic designers and software developers create computer programs. Attending hackathons can allow you to connect and collaborate with other software developers while creating working software and improving your coding experience. You can also consider putting some of the programs you complete during hackathons into your digital portfolio.
### Find opportunities at work
If you're still working while trying to become a software developer, you may find opportunities to improve your coding skills in your current career. Ask your supervisor for chances to maintain a company website, write apps for your team to use or create data storage solutions. You can also volunteer for leadership opportunities or work on additional group projects to develop general work skills, such as collaboration, organization and conflict resolution, that can be helpful in a software developer career.
### Practice your skills
Practicing your coding and software development skills can help you improve the quality of your programs. It may also prepare you for system design interviews, which require you to perform software development tasks in a set amount of time. You can find websites that offer system design problems or practice with your mentor or other software developers.
### Make a transition plan
When changing careers, consider creating a transition plan for your current job. A transition plan is a written document that lists important information for your supervisor and successor, such as recurring responsibilities, projects you're working on and useful professional contacts. Creating a transition plan can help you leave a job on good terms and show your supervisor evidence of what you've done during your time working there.Read more: [How To Write a New Job Transition Plan (With Template)](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/starting-new-job/new-job-transition-plan-template)
### Continue learning
While looking for a software development job, try to keep learning about coding and software development techniques. Increasing your knowledge may improve your coding quality and help you perform better in system design interviews. It also allows you to learn about newer programs, coding languages and techniques. Consider taking online programs or in-person classes to improve your qualifications continually.
