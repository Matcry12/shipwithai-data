#### Indicate your specialization in the digital transformation process.

You may be particularly experienced in one stage of the digital transformation process; if you are, you should indicate that. This applicant is clearly specialized in the marketing and consumer reception part of the process and they have indicated that throughout their experience section. This is particularly important if they are asking for someone specialized in the stage you are specialized in.
