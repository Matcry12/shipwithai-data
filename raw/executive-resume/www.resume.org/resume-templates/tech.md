##  Tech Resume Templates 
###  What makes a tech resume template? 
  * **Technology skills:** Tech resume templates usually feature a section that categorizes your programming languages and technical skills, allowing the hiring manager to easily parse the document for key information.
  * **Header:** In addition to your contact information, tech resume templates typically include a URL to your online project portfolio, enabling potential employers to view your most compelling projects quickly.
  * **Profile:** Tech resume templates include a summary of qualifications at the top of the resume that provides a compelling overview of your experience within the technology industry to draw the recruiter and hiring manager in.
  * **Professional experience:** When using a tech resume template, always include a robust work history section that explores the nuances of your experience within the technology industry.
  * **Education and certifications:** This layout features your technology degrees, and certifications prominently to show employers that you have the formal knowledge and training to execute complex technical projects and initiatives.
  * **Balanced visuals:** These templates offer a sophisticated, modern aesthetic that is visually appealing and conscious of page space.
  * **Customization:** Tech resume templates are easily customizable according to your individual needs, such as allowing you to prioritize skills and academic projects over your work history at the entry-level.
  * **Standard fonts:** Tech resume templates favor highly readable fonts such as Garamond, Cambria, and Arial.


###  When to pick a tech resume template 
Tech resume templates are a great choice for professionals in the technology field, but other job seekers may also benefit from the format. For instance, construction managers, videographers, and civil engineers could use this structure to categorize complex industry skills and prominently highlight project portfolios.

##  Frequently Asked Questions: General Resume Templates 
###  Do tech resumes work? 
Yes, tech resumes are usually very effective for job seekers in terms of both visual design and layout. That said, the strength of your application is always determined by the quality of your content. An appealing template won’t land you an interview if your document has uninspiring bullet points and few achievements. 
###  Do employers prefer tech resumes? 
Some tech companies and startups may prefer this template style, but hiring managers and recruiters always have unique preferences that are impossible to anticipate. The most important thing to consider with any template is whether or not it allows you to demonstrate the value you can bring to potential employers in an accessible manner. 
###  What are the benefits of a tech resume? 
The primary benefit of using a tech resume is that it allows you to present complex technical information in an easy-to-navigate way. For instance, categorizing skills allows the reader to quickly determine whether or not your mastery of specific programming languages is a good fit for their organization.
