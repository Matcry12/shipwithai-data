# How do I add skills to a Vice President Of Engineering resume?
1
Review the job posting closely.
Go through the Vice President Of Engineering posting you're applying to, and identify hard skills the company is looking for. For example, skills like Engineering Management, Software as a Service (SaaS) and Software Development are possible skills. These are skills you should try to include on your resume.
[ ![](https://resumeworded.com/assets/images/resume-guides/how-to-images/tailor-resume-to-posting.jpg?Vice+President+Of+Engineering) ](https://resumeworded.com/assets/images/resume-guides/how-to-images/tailor-resume-to-posting.jpg?Vice+President+Of+Engineering)
2
Add industry skills like Agile Methodologies and Engineering.
Add other common skills from your industry - such as Product Management, Product Development and Cloud Computing - into your resume if they're relevant.
[ ![](https://resumeworded.com/skills-and-keywords/img/vice-president-of-engineering.jpeg?Vice+President+Of+Engineering) ](https://resumeworded.com/skills-and-keywords/img/vice-president-of-engineering.jpeg?Vice+President+Of+Engineering)
3
Add skills into your work experience.
Incorporate skills - like Program Management, Distributed Systems and Solution Architecture - into your work experience too. This shows hiring managers that you have practical experience with these tools, techniques and skills.
[ ![](https://resumeworded.com/assets/images/resume-guides/how-to-images/weave-keywords-into-bullets.jpg?Vice+President+Of+Engineering) ](https://resumeworded.com/assets/images/resume-guides/how-to-images/weave-keywords-into-bullets.jpg?Vice+President+Of+Engineering)
4
Highlight technical and engineering skills.
It's important to show hiring managers your technical experience, whether that's in your previous Vice President Of Engineering roles or other experiences. Try to emphasize your technical and engineering experience through your bullet points.
[ ![](https://resumeworded.com/assets/images/resume-guides/how-to-images/engineering.jpg?Vice+President+Of+Engineering) ](https://resumeworded.com/assets/images/resume-guides/how-to-images/engineering.jpg?Vice+President+Of+Engineering)
5
Highlight any software engineering or development experience.
Programming and software development are common skill sets for Vice President Of Engineering roles, so try to highlight related accomplishments in your summary, work experience or skills sections.
[ ![](https://resumeworded.com/assets/images/resume-guides/how-to-images/software.jpeg?Vice+President+Of+Engineering) ](https://resumeworded.com/assets/images/resume-guides/how-to-images/software.jpeg?Vice+President+Of+Engineering)
6
Use the exact job title.
Try to add the exact job title, Vice President Of Engineering, somewhere into your resume to get past resume screeners. See the infographic for how to do this.
[ ![](https://resumeworded.com/assets/images/resume-guides/how-to-images/job-title-resume-title.jpg?Vice+President+Of+Engineering) ](https://resumeworded.com/assets/images/resume-guides/how-to-images/job-title-resume-title.jpg?Vice+President+Of+Engineering)
