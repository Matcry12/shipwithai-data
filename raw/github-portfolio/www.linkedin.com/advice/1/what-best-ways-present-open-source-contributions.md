##  [](https://www.linkedin.com/advice/1/what-best-ways-present-open-source-contributions#use-github-as-a-portfolio) 3 Use GitHub as a portfolio
GitHub is the most popular platform for hosting and managing open-source projects. It is also a powerful tool for showcasing your open-source contributions in a software development portfolio. You can use GitHub to create a profile that highlights your skills, interests, and achievements. You can also use GitHub to display your repositories, where you can organize your projects by topics, languages, or technologies. You can also use GitHub to demonstrate your coding style, quality, and consistency, by using tools like README files, code comments, linters, or tests. You can also use GitHub to show your communication and collaboration skills, by using features like issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions, or actions.

##  [](https://www.linkedin.com/advice/1/what-best-ways-present-open-source-contributions#update-your-resume-and-cover-letter) 5 Update your resume and cover letter
Finally, you should not forget to update your resume and cover letter with your open-source contributions. Your resume and cover letter are often the first impression you make on your potential employers or clients, so you should make sure they reflect your skills, experience, and passion for web development. You can include your open-source contributions in your resume, either under your work experience, your projects, or your skills sections. You can also mention your open-source contributions in your cover letter, either as a proof of your qualifications, a demonstration of your value proposition, or a way to express your enthusiasm and motivation. You should use keywords, action verbs, and results-oriented statements to highlight your open-source contributions in your resume and cover letter, and tailor them to the specific job or project you are applying for.
