## How to write a software engineer cover letter
These are some steps you can follow to write a cover letter when applying for a software engineer position:
### 1. Correctly format your cover letter
The first step in writing a great software engineer cover letter is to use professional formatting. Choose a word processing software you're comfortable with and create a document for your letter, using a font like Georgia or Times New Roman in a size no larger than 12 points. Aim for a formal look with 1-inch margins, or consider using cover letter templates available in your software.Related: [How To Write a Software Engineer Cover Letter (With Example)](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/cover-letter-samples/software-engineer)
### 2. Introduce yourself and greet the reader
In the first sentence of your cover letter, you can clarify your reason for writing by specifying the position for which you're applying. You can detail who you are by explaining your role and how much experience you have in it. This paragraph can include information about your specializations or interests within software engineering and how they apply to the role you want.Related: [How To Write a Cover Letter: Top 3 Tips, Format & Examples [Video + Transcript]](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/resumes-cover-letters/how-to-format-a-cover-letter)
### 3. Detail your software background
Provide information about the experiences you have that helped you gain software and [technology skills](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/career-development/in-demand-tech-skills). This can include internships, entry-level positions and personal projects. Be specific about the tasks you managed and the skills you gained in each experience.Related: [How To Write the Best Cover Letter (With Template and Sample)](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/resumes-cover-letters/best-cover-letter)
### 4. Describe your most relevant skills
While detailing your experience, you can highlight your technical and [interpersonal skills](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/career-development/how-to-improve-interpersonal-skills). Focus on hard skills, such as coding languages and Agile understanding, as well as soft skills like communication and teamwork. This approach demonstrates how you provide value to your team and the workplace. 
### 5. Include a call to action for the reader
The final paragraph of your cover letter can thank the reader for their time and prompt them to contact you with a [call to action](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/career-development/how-to-write-a-call-to-action). For example, you may express your interest in scheduling an interview or answering further questions about your candidacy. You can also include a sentence about how you can bring value to the company.
### 6. Finish and sign the cover letter
To finish your cover letter, include a sign-off and your signature. If you're submitting a digital cover letter, you can use online programs to create and add a digital signature. Consider using a professional salutation like:
  * Regards
  * Sincerely
  * With gratitude
  * Best

## Tips for writing an effective cover letter
These are some tips for writing an effective cover letter:
  * Customize each cover letter. If you're applying for multiple software engineer positions, it can be a good idea to customize a letter for each application. This can help you connect with the hiring manager and increase your likelihood of consideration for the position.
  * Be specific about your achievements. To be as specific as possible about [your achievements and impacts](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/resumes-cover-letters/professional-achievement-resume), you can include figures and timeframes. For example, rather than saying you increased sales, you may specify that you increase sales revenue by 150% in one month. 
  * Thoroughly proofread and edit your letter. Have a friend or family member review your letter or read it aloud to catch errors, and consider using grammar-checking software to identify minor issues.
  * Create a cohesive style for your materials. Consider using the same font and colors for your cover letter that you use in your resume. This can help you demonstrate your [organizational skills](https://www.indeed.com/career-advice/career-development/organization-skills) to the hiring manager.
