Resume keywords are the specific skills, tools, and terms in a job description that ATS systems look for when ranking your resume. They include hard skills (SQL, SEO), tools (Salesforce, Excel), certifications (PMP, CPA), and action verbs (Led, Optimized).

### How many resume keywords should I use?

### Where should I put keywords on my resume?

Place important keywords in your summary, skills section, and experience bullets. Use them naturally with context and measurable results, not just as a list.
