# ATS Resume Keywords for Tech, Marketing and Finance Jobs

## Written By Editorial Team

- ATS resume keywords help your resume match the skills, tools, and experience listed in a job description.
- The best way to choose relevant terms is to find keywords in job description sections like skills, responsibilities, and qualifications.
- Strong cv keywords include role-specific skills, software tools, certifications, job titles, and industry terms.

## We'll cover:

## Top ATS Resume Keywords Recruiters Search For

Recruiters and ATS systems look for resume keywords that clearly match the role, skills, tools, and qualifications listed in the job post. The easiest way to choose the right terms is to **find keywords in job description** sections like responsibilities, required skills, certifications, and preferred experience. These may include job titles, technical skills, software tools, industry terms, action verbs, and measurable achievements. For example, strong **cv keywords** could include "project management," "Salesforce," "data analysis," or "budget forecasting." You can also use a keywords finder to identify repeated terms, but the final resume should still read naturally. The best keywords for resume scanners are relevant, specific, and backed by real experience.

## Tech Resume Keywords for ATS

Tech resume keywords for ATS should reflect the exact skills, tools, and technologies used in the role you're applying for. Recruiters often search for programming languages, frameworks, cloud platforms, databases, DevOps tools, and role-specific terms. For example, a software engineer resume may include keywords like JavaScript, Python, React, Node.js, APIs, Git, SQL, and AWS. A data role may need terms such as data analysis, machine learning, Tableau, Power BI, Excel, and ETL. The key is to match your resume with the job description while keeping it readable for a human recruiter. Don't list every tool you know; include only the tech resume keywords that connect directly to your experience, projects, and measurable outcomes.

### What are ATS friendly keywords?

**ATS friendly keywords** are job-related words and phrases that applicant tracking systems can easily read and match with a job description. These usually include skills, tools, job titles, certifications, and industry-specific terms.
