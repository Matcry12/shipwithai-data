## Software Engineering Manager Cover Letter Sample (Full Text Version)

### Gary Dowsett

**Application for the position of Software Engineering Manager**

Dear Hiring Manager,

I am writing to express my interest in the Software Engineering Manager position at TTR Technologies, Inc. in Rome. I am confident that my skills and experience make me well-equipped to excel in this role and contribute to the success of your team.

In my previous role as a Software Engineering Manager at Soft Tech, Inc., I successfully managed and coordinated key software engineering functions, supervised a team of 15 employees, and implemented process improvements that led to a 24% reduction in expenses. I am a dedicated and analytical professional with a proven track record of solving complex problems.

I hold a master's degree in Computer Science from Munich University and have experience with a variety of programming languages and software programs. I am confident that my educational background and industry expertise make me a strong candidate for this position.

I have attached my resume for your review and would be happy to discuss my qualifications further. Thank you for considering my application. I look forward to the opportunity to speak with you.

Sincerely,

Gary Dowsett
