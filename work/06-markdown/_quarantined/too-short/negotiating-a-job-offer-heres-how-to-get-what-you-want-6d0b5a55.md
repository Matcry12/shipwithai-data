# Negotiating a Job Offer? Here’s How to Get What You Want.

## Summary.

When negotiating a job offer, you want to walk away with more than you had.- Whether explicitly mentioned or not, compensation packages always come with other benefits woven in. Salary is not the only negotiable.
- First

I was in the backyard when my cell phone rang with my first-ever, full-time job offer. It was summer 2007, a few months before the U.S. would enter the Great Recession. On the other end of the line was my hiring manager, sharing the happy news: I'd been selected to join the communications team at a university I admired.
