# 6 Security Manager Resume Examples for 2026

## Include your knowledge of network and web-related protocols.

Your resume should never lack important knowledge that can make you a better security engineering manager. If you know network systems and routing protocols such as IPSEC, HTTP, or BGP, always mention it in your resume. It improves your resume drastically and lets recruiters know that you are an expert in your field of work.
