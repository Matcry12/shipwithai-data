# How to Write a Director of Engineering Resume (With Example)

Updated November 20, 2025

A director of engineering has many responsibilities and holds an important position within a company. They're responsible for all aspects of the engineering department, from hiring and training new employees to preparing budgets for new projects. Understanding how to create a resume for this position can improve your chances of progressing in your field and getting hired for the role. In this article, we show you how to create a director of engineering resume, offer some tips, provide a resume template, and give you an example of a well-formatted resume to help you write your own.

## How to write a director of engineering resume

Below are steps you can follow to write your own director of engineering resume:### 1. Begin with your personal information

At the start of your resume, it's best practice to include some of your personal information. This makes it easier for the employer to identify whose resume they're reading when going through multiple applications. It can also help to make your contact information easy to find so that employers can easily call you for an interview. When listing your personal information, start with your first and last name. On the following line, list the city and province or territory you live in, your phone number, and your e-mail address. Divide all the information using pipes (|).### 2. Create a professional summary

A professional summary provides an employer with a simple way to see what you can offer the company. The statement also gives you the chance to make a compelling argument for why they may want to hire you. A great professional summary is typically one to three sentences long and summarizes your best skills, qualifications, or experience, along with an explanation as to why you want to work in the position.For a director of engineering role, you often require a high level of education and significant experience in the field. For example, your statement may say, I've worked in the field of engineering for several years and am committed to improving myself and my skills through professional experiences. I have a master's degree in engineering and enjoy discovering problems and developing creative solutions. This highlights many of your qualifications and shows a passion for engineering and learning.Related: What Does an Engineering Manager Do? (With Salary and Skills)### 3. Include your work experience

Employers typically look for a significant amount of experience when hiring for this position. If the company you currently work for wants to promote you to director of engineering, then it most likely wants you to be intimately familiar with all aspects of the field. Make sure to include all your relevant work history to improve your chances of getting the position.When listing your work history, include your job title, the name of the organization, the city and province or territory it's located in, and your start and end date. Include jobs that provide insight into each skill you bring to the job. If you've served as a project manager, that's valuable experience that can show you're used to overseeing large-scale projects and budgets.### 4. List your education history

Some jobs can have specific educational requirements, so it's important to include a detailed list of your education history. This helps to demonstrate that you meet all the educational needs the business may require. When listing your education, make sure to include the subject of your degree or diploma, the institution you attended, and the year you graduated. If you graduated more than five years ago, you can leave out the graduation date from your resume. The common educational requirements for this position are a bachelor's degree in engineering and a master's degree in business administration.Related: 8 Engineering Specializations (With Salaries and Duties)### 5. Include any additional certifications or skills

At the end of your resume, you can consider including two additional sections. One section you can consider including is certifications. When listing your certifications, include the name of the certification, the institute you received it from, and the year that you received it. The other section can be for skills. When listing your skills, put these all on the same line and separate each skill with a pipe.Including these sections can help to improve your resume and your chances of getting hired for the role. Some relevant skills for this position may be organization, attention to detail, excellent communication, and analysis.Related jobs on Indeed

## Tips for creating a resume

Listed below are some tips you can use to create a better resume:### Be honest

When employers are looking to hire someone for a position, especially a prestigious role, they're often looking for more than just experience and education. They're also interested in that person's character and personality. Honesty is an essential character trait that most employers value. Being honest can help employees create strong working relationships, allowing both the company and its employees to be more productive. Make sure that you can provide verification for any claims you make on your resume. Be prepared with examples of your skills, so you can discuss them during an interview.Related: What is Honesty and Integrity in a Workplace Setting?### Proofread your work

Submitting an error-free resume is crucial to making a good impression and progressing in the hiring process. Employers want someone who's attentive to detail, and making sure your resume is error free can demonstrate that. When proofreading your resume, look for grammatical errors, spelling mistakes, and issues with formatting. You can also read your resume out loud to help find any sentences that may sound awkward or confusing. Performing thorough proofreading of your resume may contribute to your success in a job competition.### Make it relevant

When creating your resume, ensure that all the information is relevant to the job. This means creating unique resumes for each specific role. For a director of engineering, extensive employment and education history can be pertinent to getting the position. By including only relevant information, you can make sure you properly showcase your best qualities. For example, you might mention that you're certified as a professional engineer. It may be less relevant that you're certified in first aid. Filtering out non-relevant information helps to simplify your resume and makes it easier to read.Related: 8 Areas of Improvement for Managers and Tips for Improvement### Keep it simple

Resumes are most effective when you keep the document simple and concise. Make sure that the resume is easy to read and that it properly showcases all your qualifications. Using bullet points helps to reduce wordiness and can make the resume's format more appealing.Ensuring that your resume is simple and straightforward can make it more accessible to any potential reader. This can help to improve your chances of getting an interview and moving forward through the hiring process. Being able to summarize your qualifications may also demonstrate that you can easily explain complicated information, which may be necessary for giving presentations.Related: What Is a Resume Title?
