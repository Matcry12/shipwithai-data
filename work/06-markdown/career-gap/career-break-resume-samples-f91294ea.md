# Career Break Resume Samples for Explaining Employment Gaps

Updated 13 November 2025

While your career may be a major aspect of your life, you may take a break from it for many reasons. If you're entering the workforce again after a gap in employment, it's important to address the time you took away from work on your resume. Learning how to mention career breaks when applying for jobs can help you make a positive impression on hiring managers. In this article, we explore the reasons to explain a career break in your resume and how to do it and share samples of resumes that include career breaks.

## How career break resume samples can help you write your own

Career break resume samples can help you write your own because they give you real-world examples of how to include this information on your resume. There are many ways you can explain your career break. Viewing samples can help you decide the approach you want to take on your own resume based on the reason for your break.Related: How to Explain Gaps in Your Employment in 6 StepsRelated jobs on Indeed

## Reasons to explain a career break on your resume

Explaining a career break on your resume is important because hiring managers typically want to know whether your skills are still relevant. Here are some reasons to explain your career break on your resume:- Employers may appreciate your unique perspective: Many employers are changing their opinions about career breaks, which most companies discouraged in the past. Explaining why you took a break from work shows the hiring manager how what you learned during your experience can benefit the organisation.
- You can prevent assumptions: Recruiters may make assumptions about why you have a gap in your employment history, which can lead to hiring bias. Explaining your career break in your resume can help you avoid making a negative impression when applying for jobs.
- You can highlight a variety of skills: Many professionals gain knowledge and skills while they're on a career break, either through professional development programmes or life experience. Mentioning your employment gap on your resume gives you an opportunity to highlight what you've learned.
- The interview may focus on your skills and experience: Some hiring managers take time during an interview to learn more about why you took a career break. By explaining your career break in your resume, you can shift the interview to a focus on your skills and how you might be a good fit for the role instead.

## How to explain a career break in your resume

Here are some steps you can take to update your resume to include your career break:### 1. Be honest about your experience

Regardless of the reason you chose to take a leave from work, it's important to be honest about it in your resume. You can give details or keep the information vague, depending on your level of comfort. Taking a positive approach to discussing your career break and being forthcoming with hiring managers may encourage them to view your break as a potential benefit instead of a disadvantage. For example, if you explain that you took time off to care for an ill family member, it shows the employer you have important soft skills like patience, empathy and leadership.### 2. Describe your recent career break in your summary or objective

Incorporating your career break into the summary or objective section at the top of your resume allows you to explain how it helped you prepare for a new position. You might include the skills, knowledge or experience you gained during your leave and how you plan to transfer it to a new role. It also makes your break clear to the recruiter immediately, so you can use it to make a positive impression instead of saving it for the end of your resume. This shows them you're willing to be honest and open about your experience.### 3. Remove months of employment

Removing the months during which you held jobs and took your career break can help the recruiter view your experience differently. By including months in your employment history section, you make the times you spend at certain jobs and on your leave specific. Making them more general can help the hiring manager realise the break was just a small part of your career. For example, instead of mentioning that you took a break from January 2017 through September 2018, you can just include that you were on leave from 2017 to 2018.### 4. List your career break as a sabbatical

After mentioning it in your summary or objective section, you can also include your career break in your employment history as if it were a job. Instead of a job title and company name, you might list your experience as family leave or personal sabbatical. Follow this by the years you took your career break and use bullet points to describe what you did during your employment absence. If you did freelance work or professional projects during this time, you can also add those as bullet points in this section.## Career break resume samples
